Coquitlam drivers should plan for delays at Parkway Boulevard and Panorama Drive while intersection safety work continues through late August. The notice matters most for regular neighbourhood trips that pass through the intersection.

The City of Coquitlam says its contractor is working at the intersection now. Work is scheduled Monday to Friday from **7 a.m. to 7 p.m.**, with possible Saturday work from **9 a.m. to 6 p.m.**

## What is happening

The city describes the project as intersection safety improvements at Parkway Boulevard and Panorama Drive. Its road-work notice says construction began on **June 29** and is expected to continue until late August.

Traffic will be affected during active construction. Coquitlam says drivers should expect lane closures, possible turn restrictions, and delays while crews are on site.

## What drivers should do

The practical move is to avoid the intersection when timing matters. If you need to use that route, leave extra time and watch for traffic personnel, equipment, signs, and construction speed limits.

Coquitlam's broader Traffic Hotspots page also lists current construction and says summer road work can overlap with city, Metro Vancouver, BC Hydro, and Fortis BC projects. The city recommends signing up for traffic bulletins through NotifyMe under Road Work and Construction.

This is a route-planning update, not a finished-project announcement. Check the city notice before making a regular commute plan, especially if the weather changes, crews shift the schedule, or you are timing a pickup, appointment, or connection.
