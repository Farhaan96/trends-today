**Willowbrook Drive/196 Street** north of **Fraser Highway** in Langley will have **no access to or from Fraser Highway** from **September 23 to 26, 2026**. Surrey Langley SkyTrain is installing guideway in the area, including overnight work.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-extended-hours-and-no-access-to-willowbrook-drive-196-street-north-of-fraser-highway/) posted September 10, 2026, confirms the window. Traffic pattern changes stay in effect **24/7** for those dates.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** No access to or from Willowbrook Drive/196 Street north of Fraser Highway.

**Window:** September 23 to 26, 2026.

**Hours:** Regular construction Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Overnight work will also occur. Expect noise from equipment.

**Alternate routes:** Use **64 Avenue**, **196 Street**, **Highway 10/Langley Bypass**, and **200 Street**.

**Local access:** Business access will be maintained via 64 Avenue, Willowbrook Drive/196 Street, and 200 Street.

**Fraser Highway:** Single-lane traffic in each direction will be maintained. Follow signs and traffic control.

**Transit:** Check TransLink for bus impacts in the area. Expect delays.

---

## Who is affected

Drivers who normally use Willowbrook Drive/196 Street at Fraser Highway need the posted alternates for four days, including overnight. This sits east of the Fleetwood Way daytime access closure earlier in the week and is separate from Friday’s Fraser Highway west-of-184 single-lane alternating traffic window.
