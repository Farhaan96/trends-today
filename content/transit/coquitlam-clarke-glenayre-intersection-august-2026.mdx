Road users at **Clarke Road and Glenayre Drive** should be ready for traffic delays as the City of Coquitlam carries out intersection safety improvements.

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2020) says work began the week of August 10 and will be ongoing for approximately one month.

## Where the work is scheduled

The intersection safety improvements are at one Coquitlam location:

- **Clarke Road at Glenayre Drive**

## When crews are scheduled to work

The listed schedule is:

- **Week of August 10, ongoing for approximately one month**
- **Monday through Friday**
- **7 a.m. to 7 p.m.**

## What road users should expect

The City says traffic flow will be impacted and there will be delays during active construction. Coquitlam advises road users to plan an alternate route or allow extra time.

## Construction-zone safety

The notice includes these reminders for drivers:

- Drive with extreme caution and follow construction speed limits.
- Keep a close watch for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Obey all construction signage and instructions from traffic personnel.

## How to contact the City

Questions can be directed to Coquitlam's Public Works Liaison at **604-927-3550** or **pwl@coquitlam.ca**.
