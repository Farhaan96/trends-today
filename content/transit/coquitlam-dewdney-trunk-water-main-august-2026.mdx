Road users on **Dewdney Trunk Road** between **Locarno Drive** and **Cawthra Court** should expect single-lane alternating traffic as Metro Vancouver carries out water-main construction work.

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2021) says the work began mid-August and is expected to last about eight weeks.

## Where the work is happening

The construction is part of the Metro Vancouver Dewdney Trunk Road Water Main Project:

- **Dewdney Trunk Road between Locarno Drive and Cawthra Court**

The City says access to both Locarno Drive and Cawthra Court will be maintained throughout the work. Pedestrian access is also maintained.

## What road users should expect

Coquitlam lists these traffic impacts:

- **Single-lane alternating traffic** on Dewdney Trunk Road between Locarno Drive and Cawthra Court
- Access to side streets maintained
- Pedestrian access maintained

The City advises road users to plan an alternate route to avoid delays or allow extra time when travelling through the area.

## Construction-zone safety

The notice includes these reminders for drivers:

- Drive with extreme caution and follow construction speed limits.
- Keep a close watch for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Obey all construction signage and instructions from traffic personnel.

## How to contact Metro Vancouver

Questions about the project can be directed to Metro Vancouver:

- **Metro Vancouver Information Centre**: 604-432-6200
- **After Hours Emergency**: 604-451-6610
- **Email**: icentre@metrovancouver.org (include "Dewdney Trunk Road Water Main – Mariner Way Section" in the subject line)
- **Website**: [metrovancouver.org](https://www.metrovancouver.org) (search for "Dewdney Trunk Road Water Main – Mariner Way Section" for project updates)
