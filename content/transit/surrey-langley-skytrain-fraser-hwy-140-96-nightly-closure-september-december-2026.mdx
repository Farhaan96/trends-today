**Fraser Highway between 140 Street and 96 Avenue** in Surrey transitions from full closure to nightly closures starting September 7, 2026. Road users will have daytime access restored while overnight work continues through December 20.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain updated traffic notice](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/updated-traffic-update-road-closure-and-extended-hours-on-fraser-highway-between-140-street-and-96-avenue/) posted September 1, 2026, confirms the change affects work hours for the remaining months of guideway construction near the future Green Timbers station.

---

## What road users should know

**Daytime access restored**: Fraser Highway between 140 Street and 96 Avenue reopens to traffic during the day starting September 7, 2026.

**Nightly closures continue**: The same section closes every night from **8 p.m. to 6 a.m.** through December 20, 2026. This includes weekends and statutory holidays.

**Use 96 Avenue** as the alternate route during nightly closures. Follow detour signs and traffic control personnel.

---

## Change from original notice

The September 1 update changed the overnight closure hours. The [original July 24 notice](/transit/surrey-langley-skytrain-fraser-hwy-140-96-closure-september-2026) listed overnight hours as 10 p.m. to 5 a.m. The updated notice extends work to **8 p.m. to 6 a.m.**, adding three additional hours per night.

The full 24-hour closure that began August 3 ends September 7 as originally scheduled.

---

## What to expect

Cranes and equipment will be in operation during overnight hours. Expect temporary increases in noise. The work supports delivery and lifting of large components for the future SkyTrain guideway.

---

## Who is affected

The closure affects drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians travelling on Fraser Highway between 140 Street and 96 Avenue. This area is near Green Timbers Urban Forest and Surrey Memorial Hospital.
