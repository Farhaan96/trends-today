The **loop ramp from Highway 11 northbound to Highway 1 westbound** is **permanently closed** as early as **Thursday, September 10, 2026**. Drivers who need that movement now make a **left turn at a new temporary traffic signal** and use the **existing southbound on-ramp** to Highway 1 westbound.

The [B.C. Ministry of Transportation and Transit traffic notification](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/transportation-infrastructure-projects/highway-1-fraser-valley/traffic-notifications/2026-09-04-traffic-diversions-highway-11-ramp-closure.pdf) dated September 4, 2026, posts the map and the replacement path. The climbing lane on **Highway 1 westbound at Riverside Road** is also closed under the same notice.

---

## What road users should know

**Permanent change:** Existing loop ramp from Highway 11 northbound to Highway 1 westbound is closed for good. It will be replaced later as part of interchange improvements.

**New movement:** From Highway 11 northbound, turn left at the **new temporary traffic signal** to reach Highway 1 westbound via the existing southbound on-ramp. Follow the official map and on-site signs.

**Also closed:** Climbing lane on Highway 1 westbound at Riverside Road.

**When:** In effect as early as Thursday, September 10, 2026. Timelines can shift with weather and field conditions.

**Live conditions:** Check [DriveBC](https://www.drivebc.ca/) before you travel.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone on **Highway 11 northbound** heading to **Highway 1 westbound** near Abbotsford needs the new signalized left-turn connection. Expect slower speeds through the cone zone and watch for traffic-control personnel.

This is separate from overnight Highway 1 diversions at the **264th Street Interchange** (Sept. 10 to 14, 11 p.m. to 4:30 a.m.) and the final Mount Lehman westbound overnight pattern through Sept. 11.
