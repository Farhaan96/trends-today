**David Avenue** between **Genest Way** and **Shaughnessy Street** in Coquitlam is a repaving zone starting today. The City says its contractor begins **road repaving** on **Thursday, October 22, 2026**, and the work will be ongoing for **approximately one week**. Crews work **weekdays from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.** and **Saturday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.**

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2067), posted October 21, and its [official location map](https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/20762) set out the work area, hours and traffic impacts.

The red band on the City map runs straight through the David Avenue and Pipeline Road intersection, so drivers crossing or turning at Pipeline are in the work area too, and with Saturday hours on the schedule the weekend is not a clear window either.

---

## What to know

**Where:** David Avenue from Genest Way east to Shaughnessy Street. The City map marks the approximate work area in red, crossing Pipeline Road.

**When:** Starts Thursday, October 22, 2026 and is expected to be ongoing for approximately one week.

**Hours:** Weekdays between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m., and Saturday between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m.

**Impacts:** Traffic will be reduced to a single lane within active work zones. Occasional turn restrictions may also be in place at intersections. Cyclists will be required to use the travel lane through active work zones.

---

## What to do

- Plan an alternate route to avoid delays, or allow extra travel time if you drive David Avenue between Genest Way and Shaughnessy Street.
- If you turn on or off David Avenue at Pipeline Road, watch for occasional turn restrictions and follow the signs and traffic personnel.
- If you cycle this stretch, expect to ride in the travel lane through active work zones.
- Drive with extreme caution, follow construction speed limits and keep a close watch for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Questions go to the City of Coquitlam Public Works Liaison at 604-927-3550 or pwl@coquitlam.ca.
