**Industrial Avenue and 203 Street** in **Langley City** will be **closed to all road users** on **October 1, 2026**. Surrey Langley SkyTrain is doing concrete pours and concrete work on a portion of the elevated guideway on Industrial Avenue and over 203 Street, near the future Langley City Centre Station.

The [updated Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic notice](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/updated-temporary-intersection-closure-and-extended-hours-at-industrial-avenue-and-203-street/) posted September 28, 2026, and the [official PDF with map](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS-Traffic-Update-at-Industrial-Ave-and-203-Street-Oct-1-and-4.pdf), replace the earlier September 15, 17, 25 and 26 window from the September 14 notice.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** The intersection of Industrial Avenue and 203 Street is closed to all road users on October 1, 2026.

**Hours:** Traffic pattern changes run **6 a.m. to 12 a.m.** Construction activity begins at **7 a.m.**

**Industrial Avenue pattern:** During the closure, **single-lane alternating traffic** is in effect on Industrial Avenue between **201A Street** and **203 Street**. When this work is complete, Industrial Avenue continues as **eastbound traffic only**.

**Local access:** Access to businesses and residences will be maintained. Follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Sunday work:** On **October 4, 2026**, Sunday work occurs from **7 a.m. to 9 p.m.** at the future Langley City Centre Station site and along Industrial Avenue, with **no traffic impacts**.

**Noise:** Temporary increases in noise are expected. Concrete pump trucks and deliveries will be in the work area. Lighting will be directed toward the site.

**Transit:** Check TransLink signage and [TransLink.ca](https://www.translink.ca) for bus service in the area. Expect delays on October 1.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone who normally crosses or turns at Industrial Avenue and 203 Street on October 1 needs a signed alternate during the 6 a.m. to midnight window. Local travel on Industrial between 201A and the closure uses single-lane alternating control that day only.

This is a new dated update for the October 1 back-on schedule. It is separate from the earlier Industrial/203 bulletin that covered September 15, 17, 25 and 26, and from the standing eastbound-only / pile-driving bulletin on Industrial Avenue through November.
