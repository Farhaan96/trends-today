**Fraser Highway west of 184 Street** in Surrey will run under **single-lane alternating traffic** on **Friday, September 11, 2026**, from **9 a.m. to 4 p.m.** The work supports box-beam installation for the future Hillcrest-184 Street Station on the Surrey Langley SkyTrain project.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-single-lane-alternating-traffic-on-fraser-highway-at-184-street/) posted September 9, 2026, confirms the daytime pattern. This is a one-day daytime restriction, not the separate overnight stoppages on Fraser Highway between 168 Street and 184 Street.

---

## What road users should know

**Single-lane alternating traffic:** Fraser Highway west of 184 Street runs SLAT from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on September 11, 2026.

**184 Street approach:** Lane reductions on the approach to the intersection on 184 Street.

**Pedestrians:** Access stays open on the **east side of 184 Street** between Fraser Highway and 70 Avenue. Expect delays.

**Prep vs traffic change:** Preparatory work can start at **6:30 a.m.** Traffic pattern changes do not begin until **9 a.m.**

**Follow control:** Follow signs and traffic control personnel. Expect delays in the area. Check TransLink for bus impacts.

---

## Who is affected

Drivers on Fraser Highway west of 184 Street and on the 184 Street approaches will see the biggest change during the 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. window. Pedestrians should use the east side of 184 Street between Fraser Highway and 70 Avenue.

This notice is distinct from overnight single-lane and stoppage work farther west between 168 Street and 184 Street.
