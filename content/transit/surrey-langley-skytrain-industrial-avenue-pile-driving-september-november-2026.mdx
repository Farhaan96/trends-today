**Industrial Avenue between 200 Street and 203 Street** in **Langley City** stays **eastbound traffic only through November 2026** while Surrey Langley SkyTrain continues elevated guideway work, **pile driving east of 201A Street**, and **track installation** near the future Langley City Centre Station.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain construction update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/updated-overnight-closure-and-extended-hours-on-fraser-highway-between-140-street-and-96-avenue-2/) posted September 24, 2026, and the [official PDF with work-area map](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS-Advisory-Industrial-Ave-Sep-2026.pdf), update the earlier July 6 notice. This is separate from the already-covered weekend **intersection closures** at Industrial Avenue and 203 Street on September 15, 17, 25 and 26.

---

## What road users should know

**Traffic pattern:** **Eastbound only** on Industrial Avenue from **200 Street to 203 Street**. Plan your approach if you usually drive westbound on that segment.

**Window:** In effect **through November 2026**. Timelines can change; check the project site for updates.

**Hours:** Construction can run **7 a.m. to 10 p.m.** Monday to Sunday and on statutory holidays. **Welding** may occur overnight. **Pile driving** on Sundays and statutory holidays is limited to **9 a.m. to 5 p.m.**

**Noise and vibration:** Pile driving can increase noise and vibration. The notice says you may notice items like water in glasses moving or light fixtures swaying; crews monitor active work areas.

**Walking and cycling:** Pedestrian and cyclist access is on the **north side** of Industrial Avenue.

**Local access:** Business access will be maintained. Follow signs and traffic control.

**Transit:** Check TransLink for bus impacts in Langley City near Industrial Avenue and 203 Street.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone who drives, bikes, or walks Industrial Avenue between 200 Street and 203 Street should treat eastbound-only as the standing pattern through November, not a one-day detour. Neighbours should expect intermittent pile-driving noise, including some Sunday activity inside the 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. pile-driving window.

This Monday ship covers the September 24 pile-driving and track-installation update with its official map. It does not replace earlier bulletins about short intersection closures at Industrial Avenue and 203 Street.
