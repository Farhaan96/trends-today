**Industrial Avenue and 203 Street** in **Langley City** will be **closed to all road users** on **September 15, 17, 25 and 26, 2026**. Surrey Langley SkyTrain is doing concrete pours and concrete work on a portion of the elevated guideway on Industrial Avenue and over 203 Street, near the future Langley City Centre Station.

The [updated Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic notice](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/updated-temporary-intersection-closure-and-extended-hours-at-industrial-avenue-and-203-street/) posted September 14, 2026, replaces the postponed September 10 to 11 window from the earlier September 3 notice.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** The intersection of Industrial Avenue and 203 Street is closed to all road users on the dates above.

**Hours:** Traffic pattern changes run **6 a.m. to 12 a.m.** Construction activity begins at **7 a.m.**

**Industrial Avenue pattern:** During those dates, **two-way traffic** is in effect on Industrial Avenue between **201A Street** and **203 Street**. When this work is complete, Industrial Avenue returns to **eastbound traffic only**.

**Local access:** Access to businesses and residences will be maintained. Follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Sunday work:** Sunday work through the end of September 2026 also occurs, beginning at 7 a.m. at the future Langley City Centre Station site and at 8 a.m. along Industrial Avenue, ending at 9 p.m., per the September 14 notice.

**Transit:** Check TransLink signage and [TransLink.ca](https://www.translink.ca) for bus service in the area. Expect delays.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone who normally crosses or turns at Industrial Avenue and 203 Street on September 15, 17, 25 or 26 needs a signed alternate during the 6 a.m. to midnight window. Local two-way travel on Industrial between 201A and 203 is available only during those closure dates.

This is a new dated update for the September 14 back-on schedule. It is separate from the earlier Industrial/203 bulletin that covered August and early-September dates, and from other SLS corridor notices such as 188 Street at Fraser Highway and Willowbrook Drive / 196 Street.
