**Fleetwood Way at Fraser Highway** in Surrey will have **no access to or from Fraser Highway** from **September 12 to 16, 2026**, **Monday to Saturday**, **7 a.m. to 10 p.m.** Surrey Langley SkyTrain is running a launching gantry in the area to install guideway.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-no-access-to-fleetwood-way-from-fraser-highway/) posted September 9, 2026, confirms the window. There is **no work and no traffic pattern change on Sunday, September 13, 2026**.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** No access to or from Fleetwood Way and Fraser Highway during the stated hours.

**Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m., September 12 to 16, 2026. Skip Sunday, September 13.

**Alternate route:** Use **152 Street** and **91 Avenue**.

**Local access:** Business and residential access will be maintained.

**Active travel:** Cyclists and pedestrians should follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Transit:** Check TransLink for bus impacts in the area. Expect delays.

---

## Who is affected

Drivers who normally turn between Fleetwood Way and Fraser Highway need the 152 Street / 91 Avenue alternate during weekday and Saturday work hours. Sunday, September 13, keeps normal access patterns for this notice.

This is separate from the Friday, September 11 daytime single-lane alternating traffic on Fraser Highway west of 184 Street.
