**162 Street north of Fraser Highway** in **Surrey** will have **no vehicle access to or from Fraser Highway** from **September 21 to October 12, 2026**, **24/7**. Surrey Langley SkyTrain is doing road restoration as construction progresses near the future Fleetwood Station.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-no-access-to-162-street-north-of-fraser-highway/) posted September 17, 2026, confirms the window.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** No vehicle access to or from 162 Street north of Fraser Highway.

**Hours:** The access change is in effect **24/7** from September 21 to October 12, 2026. Construction activity runs Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

**Pedestrians:** Use the **west sidewalk** to access 162 Street and 84 Avenue.

**Alternate routes:** Use **84 Avenue**, **88 Avenue**, **160 Street** and **164 Street**.

**Local access:** Business and residential access will be maintained. Follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Fraser Highway:** The official map shows single-lane traffic in each direction along Fraser Highway through the work area.

**Transit:** Check TransLink for bus impacts in the area. Expect delays.

---

## Who is affected

Drivers who normally turn between 162 Street and Fraser Highway, or who use 162 Street just north of the highway, need the signed alternates for the full September 21 to October 12 window. Pedestrians keep a west-sidewalk path to 162 Street and 84 Avenue.

This is separate from other SLS Fraser Highway notices such as 188 Street access and Willowbrook Drive / 196 Street.
