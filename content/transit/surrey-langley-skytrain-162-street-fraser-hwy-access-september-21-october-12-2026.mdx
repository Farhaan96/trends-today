**162 Street north of Fraser Highway** in Surrey's **Fleetwood** area will have **no vehicle access to or from** the street from **September 21 to October 12, 2026**. Surrey Langley SkyTrain is doing road restoration as construction progresses near the future Fleetwood Station corridor.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic notice](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-no-access-to-162-street-north-of-fraser-highway/) posted September 17, 2026, sets the window and the official map for the closure area and signed alternates.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** No vehicle access to or from 162 Street north of Fraser Highway for the full window.

**Hours:** The traffic pattern change runs **24/7** from September 21 through October 12, 2026. Construction work is **Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.**

**Alternates:** Use **84 Avenue**, **88 Avenue**, **160 Street** and **164 Street**. Follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Pedestrians:** Pedestrians can still reach 162 Street and 84 Avenue via the **west sidewalk**.

**Local access:** Access to businesses and residences will be maintained.

**Transit and delays:** Expect delays in the area. Check TransLink signage and [TransLink.ca](https://www.translink.ca) for bus service.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone who normally turns onto or off 162 Street just north of Fraser Highway needs a signed alternate for three weeks starting September 21. Local drivers and deliveries still reach businesses and homes, but through movements on that stub of 162 Street are closed.

This is a new dated September 17 update. It is separate from other live SLS corridor notices such as Industrial Avenue and 203 Street, 188 Street at Fraser Highway, and Willowbrook Drive / 196 Street.
