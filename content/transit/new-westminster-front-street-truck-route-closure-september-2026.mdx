Drivers and truckers in **New Westminster** should note that Front Street Truck Route between Begbie Street and East Columbia Street is closed for approximately four months starting Monday, August 31, 2026.

The [Pattullo Bridge Replacement notice](https://www.pattullobridgereplacement.ca/app/uploads/sites/637/2026/08/PBR-FCP-COM-NOT-20260814-Four-month-closure-of-the-Front-Street-Truck-Route.pdf) dated August 14, 2026, says the closure supports retaining-wall construction as part of ongoing Pattullo Bridge Replacement work.

## What you need to know

- **Front Street Truck Route** between Begbie Street and East Columbia Street is closed
- Closure starts **Monday, August 31, 2026**
- Estimated duration: **approximately four months**
- **Front Street Mews** remains open
- Downtown business access is maintained

## What road users should do

**Regular vehicles**: Detour via Columbia Street.

**Trucks**: Must use official truck routes. Columbia Street is excluded from truck traffic at all hours. Royal Avenue is excluded from truck traffic after 9 p.m.

Follow detour signage and traffic control personnel in the area.

## Construction activities

Construction activities will occur **24 hours a day** during this closure. The notice advises residents and commuters to expect longer travel times and increased noise during this period.

## Future closure notice

An additional Front Street Truck Route closure is anticipated in spring 2027 for Pattullo Bridge deconstruction. A separate notice will be issued for that work.
