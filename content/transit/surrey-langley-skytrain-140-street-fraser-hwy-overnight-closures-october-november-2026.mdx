**140 Street at Fraser Highway** in Surrey loses its overnight through route starting **Tuesday, October 27, 2026**. A launching gantry, the Surrey Sprinter, is installing the guideway for the future SkyTrain at 140 Street, and Surrey Langley SkyTrain says overnight and weekend work will run from **October 27 to November 8, 2026, from 8 p.m. to 6 a.m.**

The project posted a [traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-extended-hours-and-overnight-road-closures-at-140-street-and-fraser-highway/) on October 22 with a [detailed notice and map](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/10/SLS-Extended-hours-140-Street-and-Fraser-Highway.pdf).

The notice also says Fraser Highway between 140 Street and 96 Avenue will remain closed overnight, so at night the green loop on the map, 96 Avenue to King George Boulevard to 100 Avenue, is the way around the future Green Timbers Station rather than either road through it.

---

## What to know

**October 27 to November 8, 8 p.m. to 6 a.m.:** No through access on 140 Street north and south of Fraser Highway. Right turn only from southbound 140 Street to westbound Fraser Highway.

**Detour:** Northbound traffic on 140 Street will be detoured at 96 Avenue. Access to Laurel Drive is via eastbound Fraser Highway and southbound 140 Street.

**October 29 is different:** On that date the notice lists westbound traffic only on Fraser Highway between Whalley Boulevard and 140 Street, through access on 140 Street at Fraser Highway, and access to Laurel Drive via southbound 140 Street.

**Alternate routes:** 96 Avenue, 100 Avenue and King George Boulevard.

**Impacts:** Overnight access changes, intermittent stoppages of up to two minutes for deliveries, and a temporary increase in noise. The project says noise mitigation will be in place, including shutting off equipment when not in use, and lighting will be directed towards the work site. Timelines are subject to change.

---

## What to do

- If you use 140 Street or Fraser Highway near Green Timbers after 8 p.m. between October 27 and November 8, plan on 96 Avenue, 100 Avenue or King George Boulevard instead.
- Heading north on 140 Street at night, expect the detour at 96 Avenue.
- On October 29, check the notice again, since the traffic pattern that night is different.
- Follow signs and traffic control personnel on site.
- Questions go to surreylangleyskytrain@gov.bc.ca or the 24/7 information line, 1-844-815-6111.
