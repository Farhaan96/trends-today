**Surrey Langley SkyTrain has shut vehicle access to and from 188 Street on the north side of Fraser Highway in Clayton, near the future Clayton Station, for roadway restoration.** The closure holds 24/7 from November 2 to 4, 2026. Wednesday November 4 is the last day. Fraser Highway keeps a single lane in each direction, and access to businesses and homes is maintained. Use 68 Avenue instead.

The project's [official traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-no-access-to-68-avenue-188-and-189-streets-north-of-fraser-highway/), posted October 22, 2026, includes the map above, and the [weekly traffic advisory for November 2 to 8](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/weekly-traffic-advisory-november-2-to-november-8-2026/) lists the same November 2 to 4 closure at Fraser Highway and 188 Street.

---

## What to know

**Where:** 188 Street where it meets Fraser Highway, on the north side, in Clayton, Surrey (the yellow square on the map).

**When:** November 2 to 4, 2026. The access change is in effect 24/7. Crews work Monday to Saturday from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

**Detour:** Use 68 Avenue.

**What stays open:** Fraser Highway keeps one lane each way. Business and residential access is maintained. Cyclists and pedestrians should follow signs and traffic control.

**Next up:** November 5 to 10, 189 Street closes north of Fraser Highway. Use 68 Avenue again.

**Contact:** Surrey Langley SkyTrain 24/7 information line 1-844-815-6111. Timelines are subject to change.
