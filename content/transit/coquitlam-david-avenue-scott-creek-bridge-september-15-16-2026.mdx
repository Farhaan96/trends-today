**David Avenue** between **Lansdowne Drive** and **Johnson Street** in **Coquitlam** will have **single-lane alternating traffic** on **Tuesday, September 15** and **Wednesday, September 16, 2026**, from **9 a.m. to 5 p.m.** City crews are doing bridge maintenance on the **Scott Creek Bridge**.

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/2042) posted September 13, 2026, says pedestrian movements will not be impacted. Weather can shift the schedule.

---

## What road users should know

**Work:** Bridge maintenance on Scott Creek Bridge.

**Where:** David Avenue between Lansdowne Drive and Johnson Street (see the city's [approximate work-area map](https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/20571)).

**When:** September 15 and 16, 2026, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., weather permitting.

**Traffic:** Single-lane alternating traffic during active construction.

**Pedestrians:** Not impacted, per the city notice.

**Plan ahead:** Use an alternate route or leave extra time if you need this stretch of David Avenue.

---

## Driver safety reminders

The city asks road users to:

- Drive with extreme caution and follow construction speed limits.
- Watch for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Obey construction signage and traffic control personnel.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone driving David Avenue through the Scott Creek Bridge corridor between Lansdowne and Johnson on those two mid-September weekdays should expect delays in the work window. This is a short, dated bridge-maintenance bulletin, separate from longer Coquitlam corridor projects elsewhere in the city.
