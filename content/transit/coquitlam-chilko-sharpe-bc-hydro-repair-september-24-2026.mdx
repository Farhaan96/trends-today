**Chilko Drive** at **Sharpe Street** in **Coquitlam** is under single-lane traffic today while **BC Hydro** completes emergency repairs. The window runs **Thursday, September 24, 2026**, from **7 a.m. to 9 p.m.**

The [City of Coquitlam road-work CivicAlert](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2052) (posted September 24, 2026) confirms the same-day utility work and points drivers to the official location map.

---

## What road users should know

**Where:** Chilko Drive near Sharpe Street. The official aerial location map marks the approximate work area with a dashed red box at that intersection, just west of **Lougheed Highway** near **Westwood Street**.

**When:** **Thursday, September 24, 2026**, from **7 a.m. to 9 p.m.**

**Traffic:** Traffic on Chilko Drive near Sharpe Street is limited to **single lane in each direction**.

**What to do:** Plan an alternate route or allow extra time. Drive with caution, watch for work crews and heavy machinery, and follow construction signs and traffic control personnel.

**Contact:** BC Hydro toll free 1-866-647-3334.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone using Chilko Drive near Sharpe Street today needs to expect slower travel and single-lane conditions through 9 p.m. Neighbourhood traffic approaching Lougheed Highway via Westwood Street may also feel spillover delays.

This is a same-day Coquitlam utility bulletin. It is separate from the multi-week Chapman Avenue Stoney Creek sewer work farther west and from other Lower Mainland transit construction notices.
