Drivers using **Bridge Connector** and the **Royal Avenue on-ramp** to the stal̕əw̓asəm (Riverview) Bridge in **New Westminster** should plan for overnight lane closures and on-ramp closures this week.

The [Pattullo Bridge Replacement notice](https://www.pattullobridgereplacement.ca/app/uploads/sites/637/2026/08/PBR-FCP-COM-NOT-20260828-Intermittent-overnight-Bridge-Connector-lane-closures-and-Royal-Avenue-on-ramp-closures-for-finishing-works-Rev0.pdf) dated August 28, 2026, says crews are conducting finishing works on the Agnes-Royal multi-use path overpass.

## What road users should do

**Bridge Connector users**: Expect intermittent overnight single-lane traffic through the work area. One lane in each direction will be maintained. Follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Royal Avenue on-ramp users**: When the on-ramp is closed, take **6th Street to 6th Avenue**, then **McBride Boulevard to Bridge Connector**. Detour signage will be in place.

**Commercial vehicles**: Follow designated truck routes as directed.

## Bridge Connector overnight closures

Intermittent single-lane closures on Bridge Connector:

- **Started**: Night of September 1, 2026
- **Duration**: Approximately one week
- **Hours**: 10 p.m. to 5 a.m.

## Royal Avenue on-ramp overnight closures

The Royal Avenue on-ramp to the Riverview Bridge will be fully closed overnight:

- **Friday, September 4**: 9 p.m. to 5 a.m.
- **Tuesday, September 8**: 9 p.m. to 5 a.m.
- **Wednesday, September 9**: 9 p.m. to 5 a.m.

The on-ramp nights complete the overnight finishing window that started September 1 on Bridge Connector.

## Who is affected

Drivers using Bridge Connector and the Royal Avenue on-ramp should plan for delays. Emergency responders cannot use the Royal Avenue on-ramp during closures and will use the McBride Boulevard detour instead. Commercial vehicles are detoured to designated truck routes. Nearby residents may hear some construction noise. Multi-use paths are not affected.

This overnight Bridge Connector and Royal Avenue on-ramp work is separate from the [multi-month Front Street Truck Route closure](/transit/new-westminster-front-street-truck-route-closure-september-2026), which involves a different corridor and continues through late 2026.
