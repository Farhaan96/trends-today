**188 Street at Fraser Highway** will have **no access to or from Fraser Highway** from **September 14 to 19** and **September 21 to 25, 2026**, **Monday to Saturday**, **7 a.m. to 6 p.m.** Surrey Langley SkyTrain is doing construction and electrical work for new traffic signals at the intersection.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-no-access-to-188-street-from-fraser-highway/) posted September 11, 2026, confirms the window. There is **no work and no traffic pattern change on Sunday, September 20, 2026**.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** No access to or from 188 Street and Fraser Highway during the stated hours.

**Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., September 14 to 19 and September 21 to 25, 2026. Skip Sunday, September 20.

**Alternate route:** Use **68 Avenue**.

**Local access:** Business and residential access will be maintained.

**Active travel:** Cyclists and pedestrians should follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Transit:** Check TransLink for bus impacts in the area. Expect delays.

---

## Who is affected

Drivers who normally turn between 188 Street and Fraser Highway need the 68 Avenue alternate during Monday-to-Saturday work hours on the dates above. Sunday, September 20, keeps normal access patterns for this notice.

This is separate from the Willowbrook Drive / 196 Street north-of-Fraser access closure later in September, and from Fleetwood Way access changes earlier in the month.
