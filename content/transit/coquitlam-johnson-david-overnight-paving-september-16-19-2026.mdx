**Overnight paving** is scheduled at the intersection of **Johnson Street** and **David Avenue** in **Coquitlam** from **Wednesday, September 16** to **Saturday, September 19, 2026**, between **9 p.m. and 6 a.m.**

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/2045) posted September 15, 2026, says the overnight window is meant to reduce daytime traffic impacts. This is separate from the daytime Scott Creek Bridge maintenance on David Avenue between Lansdowne Drive and Johnson Street on September 15 and 16.

---

## What road users should know

**Work:** Road repaving.

**Where:** Intersection of Johnson Street and David Avenue (see the city's [approximate work-area map](https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/20584)).

**When:** September 16 to 19, 2026, overnight from 9 p.m. to 6 a.m.

**Traffic:** Detours will be in place on Johnson Street. David Avenue will be reduced to a single lane in each direction, with occasional turn restrictions.

**Plan ahead:** Use an alternate route or leave extra time if you need this intersection overnight.

---

## Driver safety reminders

The city asks road users to:

- Drive with extreme caution and follow construction speed limits.
- Watch for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Obey construction signage and traffic control personnel.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone driving through Johnson Street and David Avenue overnight during those four nights should expect detours and lane reductions. Neighbours and through traffic that normally use this Northeast Coquitlam intersection after 9 p.m. should check signed detours before they go.
