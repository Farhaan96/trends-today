**Winslow Avenue** between **Blue Mountain Street** and **Porter Street** in Coquitlam is a work zone for about the next six weeks. City crews started installing a **new water main** on **Tuesday, October 6, 2026**, and the job is expected to take **approximately six weeks**, weather dependent. Work runs **Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m.**

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2062), posted October 6, and its [official location map](https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/20739) set out the work area and hours.

The work zone ends at Porter Street, right by Como Lake Middle School on the City map, and crews start at 7 a.m., so families doing the weekday school drop-off along Winslow should expect to share the block with crews for the next six weeks.

---

## What to know

**Where:** Winslow Avenue from Blue Mountain Street east to Porter Street. The City map marks the full block in red.

**When:** Started Tuesday, October 6, 2026 and continues for approximately six weeks, weather dependent. Work happens Monday to Friday between 7 a.m. and 4 p.m.

**Impacts:** The City says traffic flow and street parking will be affected.

**Plan ahead:** The City asks drivers to plan an alternate route, drive with extreme caution, follow construction speed limits, watch for crews and heavy machinery, and obey signs and traffic personnel.

---

## What to do

- If you live on this block of Winslow Avenue, expect street parking to be limited on weekdays between 7 a.m. and 4 p.m. until the work wraps up.
- If you use Winslow Avenue as a cut-through between Blue Mountain Street and Porter Street, pick another route during work hours.
- Allow extra time for weekday morning trips near Porter Street, where the work zone ends.
- Questions go to City of Coquitlam Engineering and Public Works customer service at 604-927-3500 or epw@coquitlam.ca.
