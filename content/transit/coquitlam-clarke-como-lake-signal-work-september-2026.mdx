Drivers using **Clarke Road and Como Lake Avenue** in **Coquitlam** should expect traffic control at that intersection overnight Wednesday. The city says traffic signal commissioning is scheduled for **September 9**, weather dependent.

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/2031) says work will take place during night time hours between **8 p.m. and 2 a.m.** due to high traffic volumes in the area and overall public safety.

---

## What drivers should expect

The city says traffic signals at the intersection will be temporarily out of service during the work window. Traffic control personnel will be in place to direct road users.

Coquitlam recommends drivers plan an alternate route to avoid delays or plan extra time when using this route.

---

## Driver safety reminders

The city notice includes these reminders for road users:

- **Drive with extreme caution**: Follow construction speed limits through the work zone.
- **Watch for work crews**: Keep a close watch for personnel and heavy machinery.
- **Obey traffic personnel**: Follow all construction signage and instructions from traffic control staff.

---

## Work details

The key details for this overnight work:

- **Location**: Intersection of Clarke Road and Como Lake Avenue
- **Date**: Wednesday, September 9, 2026
- **Time**: 8 p.m. to 2 a.m. (weather dependent)
- **Impact**: Traffic signals temporarily dark; traffic control personnel on site
