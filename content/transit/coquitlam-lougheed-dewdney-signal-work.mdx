Coquitlam drivers using **Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road** should expect traffic control at that intersection overnight Thursday. The city says traffic signal equipment upgrades are scheduled for **July 23**, weather permitting.

The work is planned from **10 p.m. to 5 a.m.** During that window, Coquitlam says the traffic signal will be dark and the intersection will be managed by traffic control personnel.

## What drivers should expect

The city notice says drivers should plan an alternate route to avoid delays or plan extra time when using the intersection. The warning is narrow, but useful because the traffic signal itself is expected to be dark during the work window.

Coquitlam says drivers should travel with caution through construction zones, watch for work crews and equipment, obey traffic personnel and signs, and follow construction speed limits.
