**Surrey Langley SkyTrain has shut vehicle access to and from 68 Avenue on the north side of Fraser Highway in Clayton, near the future Clayton Station, for roadway restoration.** The closure holds 24/7 on October 29 and 30, Fraser Highway keeps a single lane in each direction, and access to businesses and homes is maintained.

The project's [official traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-no-access-to-68-avenue-188-and-189-streets-north-of-fraser-highway/), posted October 22, 2026, includes the map above, and the [weekly traffic advisory for October 26 to November 1](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/weekly-traffic-advisory-october-23-to-november-1-2026/) lists the same October 29 to 30 closure.

---

## What to know

**Where:** 68 Avenue where it meets Fraser Highway, on the north side, in Clayton, Surrey (the red square on the map).

**When:** October 29 and 30, 2026. The access change is in effect 24/7. Crews work Monday to Saturday from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

**Use instead:** 188 Street.

**What stays open:** Fraser Highway keeps a single lane of traffic in each direction. Access to businesses and homes is maintained.

**Walking and cycling:** The multi-use path on the north side of Fraser Highway may have short stops. Follow signs and traffic control staff.

## What closes next

- **November 2 to 4:** no access to or from **188 Street** north of Fraser Highway. Use 68 Avenue.
- **November 5 to 10:** no access to or from **189 Street** north of Fraser Highway. Use 68 Avenue.

The project says timelines are subject to change. Bus riders can check [TransLink alerts](https://www.translink.ca/alerts) for stop changes in the area. The project's 24/7 information line is 1-844-815-6111.
