**Holdom Avenue and Goring Street** in Burnaby has **intermittent single-lane alternating traffic on weekdays from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. during October 2026**. The Vancouver Fraser Port Authority says the lane control supports Holdom Overpass construction, and its [official map](https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/Holdom%20Goring%20Kingsland%20SLAT%20Sept.16.26.png) marks the work area in red at the intersection and along the Goring Street extension toward Kingsland Drive.

The Port Authority's [traffic impacts update](https://www.portvancouver.com/article/holdom-overpass-project-upcoming-traffic-and-construction-impacts), dated October 15, 2026, also keeps a longer traffic-pattern change in effect 24/7 from October 2 until fall 2026 on Holdom Avenue between Lougheed Highway and Kingsland Drive.

The single-lane window starts at 9 a.m., after the morning rush, so midday trips through Holdom and Goring are the ones to pad while crews work in the intersection.

---

## What to know

**Where:** Holdom Avenue at Goring Street, including the Goring Street extension toward Kingsland Drive. The Port Authority map shows the single-lane zone in red. A separate traffic-pattern change covers Holdom Avenue between Lougheed Highway and Kingsland Drive.

**When:** Single-lane alternating traffic intermittently during October 2026, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Traffic-pattern change in effect 24/7 from October 2, 2026, until fall 2026. Overnight full closures are planned for four night shifts between the evening of October 22 and the morning of October 31 (7 p.m. road closure, construction 8 p.m. to 5 a.m.).

**What stays open:** Traffic is maintained in both directions during single-lane alternating periods. Local driveway access is maintained with traffic control as needed during daytime work.

**What is closed or shifted:** Holdom Street remains closed south of Goring Street. Crosswalks at Holdom Avenue and Goring Street stay closed; pedestrians can reach Holdom Avenue via the Lougheed Highway crosswalk. No parking stalls temporarily on Holdom Avenue between Lougheed Highway and Goring Street. Cyclists northbound on Holdom Avenue between Goring Street and Lougheed Highway share the lane with vehicle traffic.

**Overnight nights (Oct. 22 to 31):** Vehicle traffic must use alternate routes via Douglas Road and Springer Avenue to reach Lougheed Highway. Holdom Avenue is local traffic only between Lougheed Highway and the closure. Bus stops on Holdom Avenue (IDs 58343 and 58342) and Goring Street (IDs 58384 and 58385) will not be served during those overnight closures. Route 133 impacts, if any, are communicated by Coast Mountain Bus Company.

---

## What to do

- Allow extra time at Holdom Avenue and Goring Street on weekdays between 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. through October, or use another route if you are only passing through.
- Expect the longer traffic-pattern change between Lougheed Highway and Kingsland Drive every day until fall 2026.
- Walkers: use the Lougheed Highway crosswalk instead of the closed Holdom and Goring crosswalks.
- Overnight of October 22 to 31: plan around the four full-closure night shifts and follow detour signs via Douglas Road and Springer Avenue.
- Transit riders: check [TransLink alerts](https://www.translink.ca/alerts) before trips that use Holdom or Goring stops overnight in late October.
- Questions: Port Vancouver Holdom Overpass project line 604-665-9004 or holdom@portvancouver.com, listed on the [project traffic page](https://www.portvancouver.com/article/holdom-overpass-project-upcoming-traffic-and-construction-impacts).
