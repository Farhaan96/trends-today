Drivers heading to **Sapperton** or **Royal Columbian Hospital** in **New Westminster** should plan for traffic changes on **East Columbia Street** through mid-November 2026.

Metro Vancouver is replacing approximately **165 metres** of Sapperton Sewer on East Columbia Street between Cumberland Street and Debeck Street. The [Metro Vancouver project page](https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/sapperton-sewer-connection-upgrade) confirms construction began the week of August 17, 2026 and runs approximately three months.

Metro Vancouver's own traffic-impacts diagram still shows a hard northbound keep-right onto Brunette plus Debeck/Cumberland cut-offs while the 165 m Sapperton sewer replacement runs. Useful for RCH and Sapperton drivers who might otherwise treat East Columbia as a normal Brunette shortcut.

## What drivers should do

**Heading to Sapperton or Royal Columbian Hospital from downtown**: Detour via McBride Boulevard to Eighth Avenue.

**Northbound on East Columbia Street**: You must turn right onto Brunette Avenue.

**Debeck Street and Cumberland Street**: No access from East Columbia Street during construction.

**Two-way traffic**: At least one lane in each direction is maintained on East Columbia Street.

## Key restrictions

- **Northbound traffic** on East Columbia must keep right onto Brunette Avenue
- **No access** to Debeck Street from East Columbia Street
- **No access** to Cumberland Street from East Columbia Street
- **Sidewalk closures** on Cumberland Street and East Columbia Street
- **Bus stop #53110** on northbound East Columbia near Alberta Street is closed

Transit riders should check [TransLink](https://www.translink.ca) for current Route 109 service updates.

## Work schedule

Construction generally runs **Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 7:00 pm**. From August 17 to September 30, bypass pumps may run outside regular hours. Metro Vancouver notes noise mitigation measures are in place.
