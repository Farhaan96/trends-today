**Left turns from 184 Street onto Fraser Highway** in Surrey are **not allowed Monday to Saturday from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. until late November 2026**. Surrey Langley SkyTrain says crews are restoring the road and doing electrical work for new traffic signals at the intersection, next to the future Hillcrest-184 St Station.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-turn-restrictions-at-fraser-highway-and-184-street/) posted October 16, 2026, says the changes are in effect now, and the [official PDF notice with map](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/10/SLS-20261016-184-St-TurnRestrictions.pdf) marks the turn restrictions where 184 Street meets Fraser Highway.

Saturdays count as a work day on this notice, so weekend errand runs out of 184 Street need the same detour as the weekday commute; only Sundays and the 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. overnight hours fall outside the posted schedule.

---

## What to know

**Restriction:** No left turns from 184 Street to Fraser Highway.

**Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

**Timing:** In effect until late November 2026. Surrey Langley SkyTrain notes timelines are subject to change.

**Alternate routes:** Highway 15, 64 Avenue, 68 Avenue, 80 Avenue and 188 Street.

**Access:** Business and residential access will be maintained. Cyclists and pedestrians should follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Delays:** The notice says the travelling public can expect delays in the area.

---

## What to do

- If you usually turn left from 184 Street onto Fraser Highway, pick one of the posted alternates (Highway 15, 64 Avenue, 68 Avenue, 80 Avenue or 188 Street) before you leave, Monday to Saturday between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m.
- Allow extra time near Fraser Highway and 184 Street through late November.
- Walking or cycling: follow signs and traffic control personnel at the intersection.
- Taking the bus: check [TransLink.ca](https://www.translink.ca) for the latest service information in this area, as the notice advises.
- Questions: surreylangleyskytrain@gov.bc.ca or the 24/7 information line, 1-844-815-6111.
