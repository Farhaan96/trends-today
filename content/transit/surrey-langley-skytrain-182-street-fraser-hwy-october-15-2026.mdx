**182 Street south of Fraser Highway** will have **no vehicle access to or from Fraser Highway** on **October 15, 2026**, from **7 a.m. to 10 p.m.** Surrey Langley SkyTrain is installing utilities for future traffic signals in the area.

The [updated Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic notice](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/updated-no-access-to-182-street-south-of-fraser-highway/) posted October 1, 2026, and the [official PDF with map](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/09/SLS-20261001-182-Street-No-access-1.pdf), set the firm October 15 window. That update follows the earlier September 18 notice.

---

## What road users should know

**Closure:** No vehicle access to or from 182 Street south of Fraser Highway on October 15, 2026.

**Hours:** 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.

**Alternate routes:** Use **64 Avenue**, **68A Avenue**, **68 Avenue**, **184 Street** and **Highway 15**.

**Local access:** Pedestrian access will be maintained. Access to residential properties will be maintained. Cyclists and pedestrians should follow signs and traffic control personnel.

**Fraser Highway:** The official map shows single-lane traffic in each direction on Fraser Highway through the work corridor. Plan extra time.

**Transit:** Check TransLink signage and [TransLink.ca](https://www.translink.ca) for bus service in the area. Expect delays on October 15.

---

## Who is affected

Anyone who normally turns onto or off 182 Street south of Fraser Highway on October 15 needs a signed alternate during the 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. window. This is a new dated update that firms the October 15 schedule that earlier weekly advisories only listed as expected.
