Drivers in the **Whalley** area of **Surrey** who use **138 Street** to get on or off **Fraser Highway** must use a different route until September 30, 2026.

The [Surrey Langley SkyTrain traffic update](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/traffic-update-access-change-138-street-north-of-fraser-highway/) posted June 30, 2026, says column construction for the elevated guideway requires a temporary traffic pattern change. The [weekly advisory](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/weekly-traffic-advisory-august-31-to-september-6-2026/) published August 28 confirms the access change remains in effect 24/7 through September 30.

With Fraser Highway itself [still fully closed between 140 Street and 96 Avenue until Sept. 7](/transit/surrey-langley-skytrain-fraser-hwy-140-96-closure-september-2026), the 138 Street access change quietly removes one of the last local ways to slip on and off the highway at the Whalley end. Treat Whalley Boulevard and 140 Street as the standing route for the rest of September rather than a one-week detour.

## What drivers should do

**No vehicle access**: There is no vehicle access to or from 138 Street and Fraser Highway during this period.

**Use Whalley Boulevard or 140 Street**: Vehicle access to the area is available via Whalley Boulevard or 140 Street.

## What cyclists and pedestrians should do

**Both sides of Fraser Highway**: Cyclist and pedestrian access on both sides of Fraser Highway is available.

**North side stoppages**: Intermittent pathway stoppages of up to 15 minutes may occur on the north side of Fraser Highway near 138 Street when equipment is in operation.

**South side recommended**: The multi-use pathway on the south side of Fraser Highway stays open and is the recommended route through the area, including to King George Station.

## Access change details

The access restriction and key dates:

- **Location**: 138 Street and Fraser Highway
- **In effect**: July 6, 2026 to September 30, 2026
- **Restriction**: 24/7 no vehicle access
- **Work hours**: Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- **Contractor**: SkyLink Guideway Partners

## Related Surrey Langley SkyTrain closures

This 138 Street access change is separate from other SkyTrain construction work in the area:

- [Fraser Highway full closure 140 Street to 96 Avenue](/transit/surrey-langley-skytrain-fraser-hwy-140-96-closure-september-2026) (through Sept. 7, then overnight closures through Dec. 20)
- [Industrial Avenue and 203 Street intersection closures](/transit/surrey-langley-skytrain-industrial-203-closure-september-2026) (Langley, specific dates in September)
