**The detour on the official map runs along Downes Road, Ross Road and Townshipline Road (the blue route).** Bradner Road itself stays open through the weekend, with single-lane alternating traffic.

The closure is part of rebuilding Bradner Road under Highway 1 for the Fraser Valley Highway 1 Corridor Improvement Program. The [traffic notification](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/transportation-infrastructure-projects/highway-1-fraser-valley/traffic-notifications/2026-10-27-bradnerrd-reconstruction-postcard.pdf) was posted October 28, 2026, on the program's [traffic impacts page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation-projects/fraser-valley-highway1/traffic-impacts).

---

## What closes next

- **Until Friday, November 6:** 24-hour single-lane alternating traffic on Bradner Road (it began Monday, October 26).
- **7 p.m. Friday, November 6, to 5 a.m. Monday, November 9:** the Bradner Road and Downes Road **south** intersection closes. The map shows that detour in green, along Lefeuvre Road and Fraser Highway.

The ministry says the work may be rescheduled if the weather is poor.
