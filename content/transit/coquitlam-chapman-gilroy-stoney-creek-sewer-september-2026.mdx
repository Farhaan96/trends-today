**Chapman Avenue** just east of **North Road** at **Gilroy Crescent** in **Coquitlam** is back under active construction for Metro Vancouver’s **Stoney Creek Sewer Upgrade**. Work resumed **September 14, 2026**, and is expected to run for **about six weeks**.

The [City of Coquitlam road-work CivicAlert](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2041) (posted September 15, 2026) confirms single-lane alternating traffic and local-access limits while crews work on this section.

---

## What road users should know

**Where:** Intersection of Chapman Avenue and Gilroy Crescent, just east of North Road. The official location map shows the approximate work area on Chapman Avenue east of North Road.

**When:** Construction resumed **September 14, 2026**, and continues for **approximately six weeks**. After Chapman Avenue work finishes, crews move **south on Gilroy Crescent** for the next phase.

**Traffic:** **Single-lane alternating traffic** is in place **24/7** along the work area. There is **no access to Chapman Avenue from Gilroy Crescent**. **Gilroy Crescent is limited to local traffic only.**

**What to do:** Plan an alternate route or allow extra time. Drive with caution, watch for crews and equipment, and follow construction signs and traffic control personnel.

**Contacts:** Metro Vancouver Information Centre 604-432-6200; after hours 604-451-6610; icentre@metrovancouver.org (subject: Stoney Creek Sewer Upgrade – Coquitlam Section). Project updates: search “Stoney Creek Sewer Project” on [metrovancouver.org](https://metrovancouver.org).

---

## Who is affected

Anyone using Chapman Avenue east of North Road, or trying to reach Chapman from Gilroy Crescent, needs to expect delays and follow signed local-access rules through late October 2026 (approximate six-week window from September 14).

This is a new dated Coquitlam utility bulletin. It is separate from the overnight Johnson Street and David Avenue paving window (September 16–19) and from other Metro Vancouver water-main work elsewhere in the city.
