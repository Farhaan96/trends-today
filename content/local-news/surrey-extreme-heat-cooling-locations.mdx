## Check these details before leaving

Use the city directory as a starting point, then confirm the trip:

1. Check the facility's same-day hours and any closure notice.
2. Confirm that everyone travelling can reach the entrance and return home safely.
3. Bring water, medication, mobility aids, and pet supplies as needed.
4. Check the [current Surrey forecast](https://weather.gc.ca/en/location/index.html?coords=49.174%2C-122.828) and active weather alerts.
5. If using transit, verify the route and stop before departure.

A person who cannot get to a listed civic facility should choose an option they can reach and return from safely rather than crossing the city in high temperatures.

## Who needs an extra check-in?

Surrey says heat can be especially dangerous for young people, older adults, outdoor workers, people with chronic heart or lung conditions, people with mental illness, people living alone, and people experiencing homelessness.

Call or visit someone at higher risk and ask concrete questions: Is their home getting hotter? Do they have water? Can they reach a cooler room or public building? Do they need a ride? A general reminder to "stay cool" is less useful than helping them choose and reach a specific place.

Call **9-1-1** for a medical emergency. Call **8-1-1** for non-emergency health advice in British Columbia, or **2-1-1** for help finding community and social services.
