Ramen Danbo has put dates on its new Main Street opening, giving Mount Pleasant another concrete food stop to plan around this week.

The ramen chain says its newest Vancouver restaurant is in soft opening from **July 15 to July 21, 2026**, with split hours from **11 a.m. to 3 p.m.** and **5 p.m. to 10 p.m.** The full grand opening is scheduled for **Thursday, July 23**, when the posted hours extend to **11 a.m. to 11 p.m.**

## What is confirmed

The official announcement calls Main Street the company's **fifth Vancouver location**. Ramen Danbo's locations page lists the address as **3982 Main Street, Vancouver, BC V5V 3P2**, with regular daily hours from 11 a.m. to 11 p.m.

The practical note is reservations. The company says it is unable to accept table reservations, so anyone trying the new location should treat it as a walk-in visit.
