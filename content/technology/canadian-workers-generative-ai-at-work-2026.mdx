**Just over one in three Canadian workers used generative AI on the job in the past year. But where you work changes the odds a lot.**

Statistics Canada asked workers aged 15 to 69 about AI in a supplement to its March 2026 Labour Force Survey. In its first release on the topic, published July 30, 2026, it found that **35.9%** of workers had used generative AI tools as part of their main job or business in the previous 12 months, "or just over one in three workers." Counting any AI or automation technology, the share was 41.6%.

## Your industry, ranked

Use of generative AI at work was highest in:

- **Professional, scientific and technical services: 65.6%**
- **Finance, insurance, real estate, rental and leasing: 59.2%**
- **Educational services: 53.0%**

It was lowest in:

- **Accommodation and food services: 16.3%**
- **Agriculture: 17.5%**
- **Transportation and warehousing: 21.1%**

By occupation, use was highest in legislative and senior management jobs (75.1%) and natural and applied sciences (67.5%), and lowest in trades, transport and equipment operators (14.7%).

## Most people use it for some tasks, not most

Among workers who used generative AI:

- **31.4%** used it daily, and **38.3%** a few times a week.
- **63.5%** used it for some but not most tasks.
- Only **11.6%** used it for most or almost all of their tasks.

## Why people don't use it

Nearly two thirds of workers (64.1%) had not used generative AI at work. The most common reason, given by **56.0%** of them, was that the tools had no applicability to their work. About 21.6% said they had no interest, and 9.8% cited security, privacy, environmental or ethical concerns.

## What the numbers can and can't tell you

- **"Used" means any use in 12 months.** It does not measure how much of anyone's job AI does.
- **It covers workers 15 to 69 in the provinces**, not the territories, people on reserves, or full-time military members.
- **It is a survey sample**, so figures have sampling error; StatCan only highlights differences significant at the 95% level.
- **More is coming.** StatCan says another supplement was planned for September 2026 and will be repeated regularly.
