Vancouver Canucks fans now have the first dates to mark for the **2026-27** season. The team says it will open on the road in Edmonton on **September 29** before returning to Rogers Arena for its home opener on **Thursday, October 1**.

The Canucks announced the full regular-season schedule on **July 16**. For local fans, the useful part is not the entire calendar at once. It is knowing when the first Rogers Arena dates and ticket windows start.

## What is confirmed for Rogers Arena

The Canucks say the October 1 home opener is against the Edmonton Oilers at **7 p.m.**. The first homestand continues with Calgary on **Saturday, October 3** and Vegas on **Sunday, October 4**.

The team also lists several later notable home dates, including Carolina on **October 20**, Toronto on **October 31**, Montreal on **February 1**, and Colorado on **April 6**.

## When ticket access starts

Single-game tickets are not all opening at once. The Canucks say regular-season single-game ticket sales begin at **10 a.m. PT** on these dates:

- **Monday, August 10**: Season Ticket Members and corporate partners
- **Tuesday, August 11**: Priority Access List
- **Wednesday, August 12**: General public

Fans who care about the home opener or the early Oilers, Flames, and Golden Knights dates should verify the full schedule and ticket rules through official Canucks links before buying. The practical next step is to check the complete schedule, decide which Rogers Arena dates matter, and note the August ticket window that applies to you.
