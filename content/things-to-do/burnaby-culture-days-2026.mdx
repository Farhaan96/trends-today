## Why this planner exists

Culture Days is easy to miss if you only see a national calendar. Burnaby concentrates free drop-ins around **Deer Lake**: the Village Museum, the Art Gallery, and the Shadbolt Centre. One dated free day is enough for most families. World Rivers Day on **September 27** is the clearest still-ahead pick after mid-September weekends have already run.

This guide is based on the City of Burnaby event pages checked the morning of **September 22, 2026**. Hours and weather moves can change. Treat City pages as the live source.

If you already liked free outdoors planners such as [Lynn Canyon](/things-to-do/lynn-canyon-day-trip-guide-2026) or a village walk like [Deep Cove](/things-to-do/deep-cove-day-trip-guide-2026), Culture Days adds indoor craft and museum options on the same Deer Lake campus without a ticket booth for the main celebration day.

## World Rivers Day at Burnaby Village Museum

**When:** Sunday, September 27, 2026, **11 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**  
**Where:** Burnaby Village Museum, **6501 Deer Lake Ave**  
**Format:** Drop-in. **No registration required.**

The City describes World Rivers Day as a free annual celebration with origins in Burnaby. Guests get family-friendly demonstrations, displays, tours, activities, crafts, and live entertainment across the museum grounds. Partnerships include environmental community groups, performers, and Indigenous educators.

Program notes listed by the City for the day include:

- Indigenous games, arts, and crafts for the whole family
- A river-themed scavenger hunt across the museum grounds
- History of Burnaby creeks, streams, and waterways with nature photographer and Knowledge Keeper John Preissl and artist Laurie Beeman
- Live kids entertainment and music on the Vorce Tram stage
- Environmental documentary screenings

**Carousel:** The Culture Days page lists carousel rides at **$2.71 + tax**. Everything else on the celebration day is framed as free site access and drop-in programming. Confirm the fee on the City page the morning you go.

**Phone:** 604-297-4565 (City listing for the museum).

### Ecosystem restoration volunteer days (registration)

If you want a hands-on stewardship option tied to the same rivers theme, the City lists Green Teams of the Lower Mainland and Byrne Creek Streamkeepers cohosted restoration days:

- **Byrne Creek Ravine:** Sunday, September 20, 9:45 a.m.-1 p.m. Already past as of this publish. Skip.
- **Charles Rummel Park (Eagle Creek):** Saturday, **September 26**, 9:45 a.m.-1 p.m. Registration required. Equipment provided. All ages and experience levels welcome per the City page.

Register through the City’s World Rivers Day listing if you want the Sep 26 slot. Do not show up assuming drop-in.

## Other Culture Days free drop-ins still ahead

Burnaby’s Culture Days page clusters free experiences at three Deer Lake venues. Below are activities that were still ahead when this article was checked on **September 22**. Skip anything that already ran on September 20.

### Burnaby Art Gallery (6344 Deer Lake Ave)

- **Sunday, September 27, 1-4 p.m.: In the BAG (Barn Studio).** Drop-in making session responding to the current exhibition RESIST!. Friends, family, or solo. Same day as World Rivers Day, so you can stack museum morning and gallery afternoon if energy allows.
- **Sunday, September 20: Storytelling with Julian Brave NoiseCat.** Already past. Skip.

### Shadbolt Centre for the Arts (6450 Deer Lake Ave)

- **Monday-Tuesday, September 28-29, 4-6:30 p.m.: Zine-Making Zone (Atrium).** Led by Logaine Navascués. Family-friendly for ages 8 and up. Write, draw, cut, and fold one-page zines.
- **Tuesday, September 29, 1:30-3 p.m.: The Vault: Soda Kiln Unload & Atmospheric Introduction (Steam Plant).** Ceramic technician tour coincides with unloading the Vault soda kiln. May be cancelled if raining.
- **Saturday, October 3, 10:30 a.m.-1 p.m.: Sketching with Ian De Hoog, Broderick Wong & Urban Sketchers Vancouver.** South Plaza and Deer Lake Park (Atrium if raining hard). Bring a sketchbook if you have one. Materials provided for those who need them.
- **Saturday, October 3, 1:30-3:30 p.m.: Art on the Spot (Atrium).** Sol Chiniquay biodata sonification demonstration and Louise Reimer clay responses.
- **Saturday, October 3, 1-3:30 p.m.: BAG on a Bike.** Mobile printing press near the Shadbolt Plaza / Burnaby Art Gallery area. Make a print.
- **Saturday, October 3, 3:30-5 p.m.: Modern Dance with Raven Spirit’s Starr Muranko.** Adults, all levels. Wear clothes you can move in. Bare feet. Registration was listed to open September 12. Confirm on the City page.
- **Tuesday, October 6, 6:30-9 p.m.: Jazz Jam (Studio Theatre).** Hosted with Cory Weeds of the Cellar Jazz Society. Free and informal for musicians of all ages and abilities. Note: Oct 6 sits just after the national Culture Days window end date of Oct 4, but the City still lists it on the Culture Days page.

**Already past (do not plan):** September 20 Burnaby Potters’ Guild Tips & Tricks at Mathers House.

Inclement weather note from the City: some outdoor activities may move inside. Recheck the morning of your pick.

For a broader national pointer, the City links [Culturedays.ca](https://www.culturedays.ca/). Use that as secondary. Burnaby venue hours and fees come from burnaby.ca.

## How to get to Deer Lake

Deer Lake’s arts cluster (Village Museum, Art Gallery, Shadbolt) sits south of Canada Way near Sperling / Deer Lake Avenue. Exact bus patterns change. Use [TransLink Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/) the morning you go and treat travel times as **typical**, not guaranteed.

**Driving and parking:** Follow City or museum parking signs for Deer Lake Park venues. Lots fill on sunny weekend event days. Arrive early for World Rivers Day if you are bringing kids and want scavenger-hunt time before crowds peak.

**On foot between venues:** The Art Gallery, Shadbolt, and Village Museum are short walks apart on the Deer Lake campus once you are on site. That is the point of stacking World Rivers Day with In the BAG the same afternoon if you want two free stops without moving the car.

Confirm any temporary path closures on City park pages if you are combining a lakeside walk with the museum day.

## Pairing ideas (other days, not same-day force)

Culture Days is a free arts window, not a forced all-day grind. Sensible stacks on **different** days:

- A North Shore free outdoors half day at [Lynn Canyon](/things-to-do/lynn-canyon-day-trip-guide-2026)
- A harbour village walk in [Deep Cove](/things-to-do/deep-cove-day-trip-guide-2026) or [White Rock](/things-to-do/white-rock-day-trip-guide-2026)
- A car-free island half day on [Bowen](/things-to-do/bowen-island-day-trip-guide-2026) when ferry buffers work for you

Do not try to glue Night Market hours onto World Rivers Day as a same-evening must. Pick one free Deer Lake anchor, then leave room for weather and kid energy.
