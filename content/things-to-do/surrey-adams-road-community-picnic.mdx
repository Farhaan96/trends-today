[The City of Surrey's event listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/community-picnic-adams-road-park) says a free Community Picnic at Adams Road Park is scheduled for Thursday, July 30.

For Surrey families, the useful value is a low-commitment park evening: bring food, let children try self-directed games through Park Play, and know that the City says the first **200** people at each picnic in the series can receive a free food item.

## What is confirmed

The City lists the core event details this way:

- **Date**: Thursday, July 30, 2026
- **Time**: 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.
- **Price**: Free
- **Location**: Adams Road Park
- **Address**: 18160 68 Ave.

The listing describes the event as a chance to get outdoors with friends and family, bring a picnic meal, learn about City services, and celebrate summer.

## What families can expect

The City says visitors can pack a picnic and join community networking, activities, and games. It also points attendees to Park Play, which offers self-directed park activities for families.

What that means for planning:

- **Pack a picnic** because the City frames the event around bringing a picnic meal.
- **Arrive near the start** if the first-200 food-item note matters to your group.
- **Expect outdoor games** and borrowed sports equipment through Park Play.
- **Look for City and community partners** if you want to connect with local services while there.

## The series continues in August

The City page also lists four later Thursday picnics, all from **5:30 to 7:30 p.m.**:

- **Aug. 6**: Hawthorne Rotary Park, 10513 144 Street
- **Aug. 13**: Maple Green Park, 8959 150 Street
- **Aug. 20**: Strawberry Hill Park, 7676 122 Street
- **Aug. 27**: Guildford Heights Park, 10310 154 Street

The cleanest plan for July 30 is to pack a simple picnic, use the Adams Road Park address, and treat the food item as limited rather than a full dinner plan.
