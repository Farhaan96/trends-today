**How do you do a Steveston day trip from Vancouver?** Take the Canada Line south and connect by bus toward Steveston, or drive about 30 minutes depending on traffic. Walk Fisherman's Wharf first, eat fish and chips early to miss the longest lunch line, then choose Gulf of Georgia Cannery or Britannia Shipyards, and finish at Garry Point Park. The 2026 Steveston Farmers and Artisan Market season has ended (last listed date was Sunday, Sept. 13). Confirm museum hours and transit times before you leave.

## Why Steveston works as a half-day trip

Steveston still feels like a working harbour more than a theme park. You get boats at the floats, a short village grid on Moncton and Bayview, two serious heritage sites within walking distance, and Garry Point Park for a wind-off-the-water finish. That mix fits a half day without forcing you into a packed itinerary.

Set expectations. Sunny weekends get busy. Fish-and-chips lines stretch. Parking near the wharf fills. If you arrive late morning, eat a little early, and keep one indoor backup in your pocket, the day stays calm.

## How to get there

### Canada Line + bus

The clean public-transit pattern is **Canada Line south to Richmond-Brighouse Station**, then a **bus toward Steveston**.

Two common bus options from Brighouse:

- **401** (One Road / No. 1 Road corridor toward Steveston)
- **406** (Railway Avenue toward Steveston)

The bus leg is often about **18 to 25 minutes** in normal conditions. Door to harbour from downtown Vancouver is often about **45 to 70 minutes**, depending on waits and transfers. Treat those as **typical**, not guaranteed. Check [TransLink Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/) or the Transit app for live departures on your day.

Britannia Shipyards' own visit page also notes buses such as 402, 403, or 407 to the Railway and Moncton area, then about a 10-minute walk to the shipyards. If your main stop is the village wharf, stay on a Steveston-bound 401 or 406 and walk from the exchange.

### Driving and parking

From Vancouver, driving is often about **25 to 40 minutes** depending on the bridge and traffic. Lots near the wharf and village fill first on sunny weekends. Street parking turns over slowly. If you are driving, aim for a late-morning arrival so you are not hunting for a stall at peak lunch.

Paid parking sits at the Gulf of Georgia Cannery. The village has limited free street parking. Britannia Shipyards lists free parking in two lots plus street parking at 5180 Westwater Drive.

## Hour-by-hour half-day outline

Use this as a template, not a stopwatch.

**10:30-11:00 a.m.** Arrive at Steveston Exchange or a village lot. Walk to the harbour along Bayview or Third Avenue.

**11:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m.** Fisherman's Wharf boardwalk. Watch the boats. Check the fish sales float if boats are selling. Decide on lunch before the noon crush.

**12:00-1:15 p.m.** Fish and chips or another seafood stop. Eating a bit early usually beats waiting through the peak line.

**1:15-3:00 p.m.** Heritage block. Pick **one** main indoor stop so you are not rushing both: Gulf of Georgia Cannery *or* Britannia Shipyards. Save the other for a return visit if you have energy.

**3:00-4:30 p.m.** Walk west to **Garry Point Park**. Loop the paths, sit on the grass, and catch the river mouth light. Grab coffee or ice cream on Moncton Street as a buffer before you head back.

If rain shows up, flip the order: heritage first, then a shorter Garry Point walk when the showers ease.

## Fisherman's Wharf and seafood

Fisherman's Wharf is the emotional centre of the visit. The boardwalk runs along the harbour. Working boats come and go. On some days you can buy fish or prawns directly from boats at the sales float. Bring a payment method that works for small vendors, and ask before you assume hours or cash rules.

**Pajo's at the Wharf** is the long-running floating fish-and-chips spot at the corner of Bayview and Third Avenue (12351 3rd Avenue). The official page tells visitors to check Google for current hours or call **604-272-1588**. We are not inventing a ranking or a "best of" score. It is a well-known wharf option with outdoor seating and a seasonal outdoor footprint. Confirm it is open on your day before you build lunch around it.

Other seafood and casual spots sit along Bayview and Moncton if the first line looks long. The practical move is simple: look at the line, decide early, and eat. Waiting until 1:30 p.m. on a sunny Saturday is how a half day turns into a queue.

## Heritage and indoor backups

### Gulf of Georgia Cannery

The [Gulf of Georgia Cannery National Historic Site](https://gulfofgeorgiacannery.org/visit/hours-admissions/) sits at **12138 Fourth Avenue**, right in the village. It tells the West Coast salmon-canning story inside a real industrial building over the water.

According to the Cannery's hours and admissions page (checked Sept. 14, 2026):

- **Hours:** open daily, **10 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- **Adults:** $14.75
- **Seniors (65+):** $12.50
- **Youth (17 and under):** free
- **Society members:** free

Parks Canada's summer **Canada Strong Pass** free-admission window at participating sites ran **June 19 through September 7, 2026**. That window has ended. Regular fees apply again as of this guide. Admissions are valid for the full day with same-day re-entry. The building can feel cooler than the street outside. Dress for it. Food and drink are not allowed inside the museum.

### Britannia Shipyards

[Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site](https://stevestonheritage.ca/visit/britannia-shipyards-national-historic-site/plan-your-visit/) is an eight-acre heritage park on the Fraser at **5180 Westwater Drive**, about a 15-minute walk (or a short bike or drive) east of the village centre.

From the official plan-your-visit page (checked Sept. 14, 2026):

- **Outdoor park:** dawn to dusk
- **Fall building hours (Sept. 8 to Oct. 5, 2026):** Tuesday to Sunday, **10 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- **Closed Mondays** in that fall window
- **Admission:** free entry to the heritage buildings
- **Note:** the Seine Net Loft remains closed for repairs; other heritage buildings are open when the site is operating

Winter hours later in the year shrink to Fridays through Sundays. If you visit after Oct. 5, re-check the site before you go. Free parking is available in two lots and on the street.

## Garry Point Park and village streets

Garry Point Park is the reset button at the end of the day. Wide grass, river mouth views, and enough wind that kites often appear when conditions cooperate. Paths loop the point. It is an easy place to sit without a schedule.

On the walk back into the village, Moncton Street works as buffer time: coffee, a bakery stop, or ice cream. Bayview keeps you closer to the water if you want one more harbour look before the bus or car ride home.

## If you were hoping for market Sunday

The **Steveston Farmers and Artisan Market** ran at **Garry Point Park** in 2026 (7th Avenue between Chatham and Moncton), typically **10 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.** on listed Sundays.

Official SFAM and Steveston Community Society listings for 2026 were: May 10 and 24; June 14 and 28; July 12 and 26; August 2, 16, and 30; and **September 13**.

**As of Monday, September 14, 2026, that season's listed dates are complete.** The last market Sunday was Sept. 13. Do not plan a 2026 market stop around this guide. Watch [sfam.ca](https://sfam.ca/) for any future season announcements.

## Pairing ideas

If you still have energy after Garry Point and the [Richmond Night Market final weeks](/things-to-do/richmond-night-market-final-weeks-2026) season is open, you can stack an evening at **8351 River Road** near Bridgeport. That 2026 night-market run is scheduled through **Sunday, September 20**. Gates typically open at 7 p.m. Keep it optional. A Steveston half day plus a full night market is a long stretch for kids or anyone who wants an early night.

For a lighter add-on, stay in Richmond: dinner along No. 3 Road, or a quiet Canada Line ride home while the light fades over the Fraser.

## Practical checklist before you leave

- Confirm Canada Line and bus 401/406 times in the Transit app
- Confirm Cannery hours and admission on [gulfofgeorgiacannery.org](https://gulfofgeorgiacannery.org/visit/hours-admissions/)
- Confirm Britannia fall hours on [stevestonheritage.ca](https://stevestonheritage.ca/visit/britannia-shipyards-national-historic-site/plan-your-visit/) (closed Mondays through Oct. 5, 2026)
- Call or check listings for fish-and-chips hours the morning you go
- Pack a layer. Harbour wind and the Cannery interior both run cooler than a sunny Moncton Street sidewalk

Steveston rewards a simple plan: arrive without rushing, eat before the line peaks, pick one heritage stop, and give Garry Point the last word.
