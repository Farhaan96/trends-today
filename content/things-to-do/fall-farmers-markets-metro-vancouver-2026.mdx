## Saturday markets

### Trout Lake Farmers Market

**When**: Saturdays, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., through Oct. 31, 2026

**Where**: Lakewood Drive and East 13th Avenue, at John Hendry Park

Trout Lake is one of the largest and longest-running Vancouver Farmers Markets locations. Expect a wide range of produce, baked goods, prepared foods, and artisan vendors. The market sits on the east side of the park, with easy access from the Commercial-Broadway area.

**Transit tip**: Take the SkyTrain to Commercial-Broadway Station and walk east along East 12th Avenue (about 15 minutes) or catch the 20 bus eastbound.

**Official page**: [eatlocal.org/markets/trout-lake/](https://eatlocal.org/markets/trout-lake/)

---

### Riley Park Summer Market

**When**: Saturdays, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., through Oct. 31, 2026

**Where**: 50 E 30th Ave & Ontario St

The Riley Park summer market is smaller and more neighbourhood-focused than Trout Lake. It works well for a quick stop if you are already in the Main Street or Cambie corridor.

**Note**: Riley Park Winter Market resumes around Nov. 7, 2026. Check the official page for the exact transition date.

**Official page**: [eatlocal.org/markets/riley-park/](https://eatlocal.org/markets/riley-park/)

---

### West End Farmers Market

**When**: Saturdays, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., May 2 through Oct. 31, 2026

**Where**: 1100 Comox Street (Comox and Thurlow)

The West End market is the most central option for downtown visitors. It draws a mix of local produce and prepared foods in one of Vancouver's densest residential neighbourhoods.

**Transit tip**: Walk from Burrard Station (about 10 minutes) or any downtown bus route along Robson or Davie.

**Official page**: [eatlocal.org/markets/west-end/](https://eatlocal.org/markets/west-end/)

## Sunday markets

### Kitsilano Farmers Market

**When**: Sundays, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., through Nov. 1, 2026

**Where**: Larch Street and West 10th Avenue, in the Kitsilano Community Centre parking area

Kitsilano is a popular Sunday destination with a strong selection of local farms and food vendors. The market wraps up one day later than the Saturday markets (Nov. 1 instead of Oct. 31).

**Winter handoff**: Kitsilano Winter Market typically resumes around Nov. 8, 2026. Check [eatlocal.org](https://eatlocal.org/markets/kitsilano/) for the confirmed schedule.

**Transit tip**: Take the 99 B-Line to Arbutus and walk south to West 10th, or the 9 bus along Broadway.

**Official page**: [eatlocal.org/markets/kitsilano/](https://eatlocal.org/markets/kitsilano/)

---

### Mount Pleasant Farmers Market

**When**: Sundays, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., through Nov. 1, 2026

**Where**: Dude Chilling Park, 8th Ave & Guelph St

Mount Pleasant is a walkable option for residents of the Main Street and Fraser Street corridors. Like Kitsilano, it extends one day past the Saturday markets.

**Official page**: [eatlocal.org/markets/mount-pleasant/](https://eatlocal.org/markets/mount-pleasant/)

## How to visit without wasting the trip

A trip to a farmers market can go sideways if you arrive after vendors have packed up or do not have the right supplies. Here is how to get it right:

**Bring your own bags.** Most vendors do not provide plastic bags. A reusable tote or backpack makes a difference.

**Bring cash and a card.** Most vendors accept credit and debit (tap is common), but some cash-only vendors still operate. A mix of payment options covers you.

**Arrive in the first hour for the best selection.** By 1 p.m. at a 2 p.m.-close market, popular items (baked goods, specialty produce) are often sold out.

**Check the official page the day before.** Markets occasionally close early for weather or holiday schedules. A quick check on [eatlocal.org](https://eatlocal.org/markets/) saves a wasted trip.

**Plan for parking or transit.** Weekend markets near busy parks (Trout Lake, Kitsilano) can have limited parking. Transit is often faster than circling for a spot.
