**Can you do a Trout Lake day trip from Vancouver without a car?** Yes. Use [TransLink Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/) for your door-to-park trip. Ride SkyTrain to **Commercial-Broadway Station** (Expo or Millennium Line) and walk south on **Victoria Drive** toward **East 19th Avenue**, or ride Expo Line to **Nanaimo Station** and walk west. Bus **#20** (**Victoria**) also serves Victoria Drive beside the park. Walk the lake paths, picnic areas, and the north **off-leash** area. Prefer transit when community-centre and park stalls are paid or full. Confirm Trip Planner times and **posted beach and park signs** before you leave.

## Why Trout Lake works as a half day

Trout Lake is East Vancouver's freshwater lake park: paths, picnic tables, sports fields, a community centre, and a large dog off-leash area at the north end. You can ride SkyTrain or the Victoria Drive bus and walk without gambling on a scarce stall.

That is the point of this planner: one park, clear transit, beach and water-quality honesty.

Set expectations. **Do not plan on swimming.** The City of Vancouver Trout Lake Beach page states there are **no lifeguards on site** and **swimming is not permitted**. A baseline water-quality study under the John Hendry Park Renewal Plan is underway until the **end of 2027**. Potable-water top-ups into the lake have been discontinued to support that study, which may lower lake levels in dry months. Confirm live signs the morning you go.

If you liked the transit-first lake day of [Belcarra](/things-to-do/belcarra-regional-park-day-trip-guide-2026), this is the in-city freshwater sibling. For a North Shore canyon contrast later, pair with [Capilano River](/things-to-do/capilano-river-regional-park-day-trip-guide-2026) or [Pacific Spirit](/things-to-do/pacific-spirit-regional-park-day-trip-guide-2026).

## How to get there without a car

### SkyTrain, then walk (preferred)

**Commercial-Broadway Station** (Expo and Millennium Lines) is the usual door. Exit toward Victoria Drive / Commercial Drive and walk south on Victoria Drive toward East 19th Avenue and the park. Walking time is typically about **10 to 15 minutes** from the station area. Treat that as **typical**, not guaranteed.

**Nanaimo Station** (Expo Line) is another Expo option. Walk west toward the park. Build buffer for lights and hills.

Use [TransLink Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/) for the exact door-to-park trip and return.

### Bus #20 Victoria

[TransitDB lists route **20** as Victoria](http://www.transitdb.ca/route/20/). Useful park-area stops from public TransitDB listings:

- **Northbound Victoria Dr @ E 22 Ave** (stop **51215**)
- **Southbound Victoria Dr @ E 22 Ave** (stop **50970**)
- Nearby corridor stops include **Northbound Victoria Diversion @ Victoria Dr** (stop **51216**) and **Southbound Victoria Diversion @ Commercial St** (stop **50967**)

Confirm Trip Planner headsign and stop before you leave. Night bus **N20** also appears on some Victoria Drive stop pages. Do not invent fixed downtown arrival minutes.

### Bike notes

Cycle to the park on signed street routes, then walk lake paths if bikes are restricted on a segment. Obey posted park signs. Do not invent an off-street protected path the whole way.

## Driving and parking (if you skip transit)

Prefer SkyTrain or the bus so the half day is not a parking hunt. City of Vancouver community-centre pages note a **paid parking pilot** beginning **mid-February 2026** at Hillcrest, Kerrisdale, and **Trout Lake** community centres and adjacent park spaces, with free parking for community-centre users under posted rules.

Sunny weekend stalls near the beach and fields fill. Read the meter and sign text on the stall you use. Do not leave valuables visible.

## Hour-by-hour half-day outline

1. **Arrive by SkyTrain or #20.** Alight at Commercial-Broadway or Nanaimo, or on Victoria Drive near East 22nd / the park frontage. Walk to the south beach corridor at Victoria Drive and East 19th Avenue.
2. **South shore block (45-75 minutes).** Picnic tables, washrooms, play fields, and lake views. Stay out of the water. No swimming. No unauthorized boats or fishing.
3. **Lake loop or north end (45-90 minutes).** Walk signed paths toward the north end for the **dog off-leash** area if that is your group. Keep dogs under control and scoop waste. Yield to other path users.
4. **Optional Saturday market (60-90 minutes).** Vancouver Farmers Markets lists Trout Lake **Saturdays 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., April 4 to October 31, 2026**, at **Lakewood Drive and East 13th Avenue**. Confirm the live market calendar. Bring a tote. Do not invent vendor lists.
5. **Optional community centre.** Trout Lake Community Centre is at **3360 Victoria Drive** (City page). Check facility schedules directly if you want a drop-in program. This guide does not invent class times.
6. **Return transit.** Walk back to Victoria Drive or to Commercial-Broadway / Nanaimo with buffer. Confirm Trip Planner.

Swap market and lake blocks if you arrive mid-morning on a Saturday.

## Beach, dog, and water-quality honesty

City of Vancouver Trout Lake Beach notes (checked Oct. 1, 2026):

- **No lifeguards on site. Swimming not permitted.**
- **Not permitted:** fishing, or boats / watercraft on the lake, without authorization.
- Features listed: play fields, concession, public washrooms, picnic area, barbeques permitted, dog off-leash area (north end of lake).
- A **baseline water-quality study** is underway until the **end of 2027** as part of the John Hendry Park Renewal Plan. Fresh potable water additions into the lake have been discontinued to support the study. Lake levels may drop in dry months.

Read the signs at the beach the morning you go. Rules can change with health or operations notices.

**Dogs:** use the signed north off-leash area. Elsewhere, expect leash rules unless a sign says otherwise. Scoop and dispose of waste. Keep dogs out of restricted beach or sports zones.

## What not to confuse with this park

**Trout Lake Community Centre** programs (arena, fitness, classes) are facility bookings with their own schedules and fees. A park day trip does not require a membership.

**Other Metro Vancouver "trout" or lake days** (for example Sasamat Lake at Belcarra) are different parks with different transit and swimming rules. Do not mix their beach status with John Hendry Park.
