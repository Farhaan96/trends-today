Pumpkin patches near Vancouver open in mid-to-late September. Most run through Halloween. A few stretch into the first week of November.

## Laity Pumpkin Patch in Maple Ridge opens Sept 26. Maan Farms in Abbotsford opens Sept 12. Greendale Acres corn maze in Chilliwack runs through Oct 31, with a final day on Nov 1. Aldor Acres in Langley opens Sept 19. Check official farm pages for current hours before you drive.

This guide covers six farms with verified 2026 schedules and ticket prices. We pulled all details from official websites on Sept. 8, 2026.

## Quick picker: which farm fits your group

**Toddlers and younger kids:** Laity Pumpkin Patch North (Maple Ridge) is built for ages 0 to 6. Aldor Acres (Langley) spreads animal areas across five acres with hands-on contact.

**Older kids and maze fans:** Laity South (Maple Ridge) suits older children and has attractions for all ages. Maan Farms (Abbotsford) runs two corn mazes. Greendale Acres (Chilliwack) has Canada's original corn maze at about 12 acres.

**Shortest drive from Vancouver:** Hazelmere in South Surrey and Aldor Acres in Langley are both roughly 45 minutes from downtown.

**Photo opportunities:** Maan Farms has 15 photo spots in its forest pumpkin patch, including a pumpkin house billed as Canada's biggest. Taves Family Farms (Abbotsford) has a princess carriage and storybook displays.

**Longest season:** Maan Farms runs Sept 12 to Nov 1. That gives you the most flexibility if your schedule is tight.

## Maple Ridge: Laity Pumpkin Patch

Laity Pumpkin Patch has operated for over 30 years. The farm runs two locations across the street from each other.

**North Location**
- **Address:** 21145 128th Avenue, Maple Ridge
- **Dates:** Sept 26 to Oct 31, 2026
- **Best for:** Ages 0 to 6

**South Location**
- **Address:** 12725 Laity Street, Maple Ridge
- **Dates:** Sept 26 to Oct 31, 2026
- **Best for:** Older kids and all ages

**Ticket prices (2026):**

- Weekends and holidays: $16 (ages 3 and up)
- Weekdays: $13 (ages 3 and up)
- Children 2 and under: Free with a paying adult

**What to know:**

- Tickets are sold at the entrance gates only. No online tickets in 2026.
- North has attractions scaled for small children. South has bigger rides and activities.
- School tours run weekdays in October by reservation.
- Expect crowds on weekends. Arrive early.

**Drive time from Vancouver:** About 50 minutes via Highway 1 and Lougheed Highway.

**Official site:** [laitypumpkinpatch.com](https://laitypumpkinpatch.com/)

## Langley: Aldor Acres Family Farm

Aldor Acres is a third-generation, 80-acre working farm. The fall pumpkin patch runs daily from Sept 19 to Oct 31, 2026.

**Address:** 24990 84 Ave, Langley

**Hours:** 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily (last entry at 5 p.m.)

**What to expect:**

- Pumpkin fields with various shapes, sizes, and colours
- Open-air animal interactions across five acres (pigs, goats, donkeys, chickens, sheep, bunnies, emus, horses, cows, and more)
- Hayrides and forest walks
- A hay-jumping pile
- Farm market with homegrown and locally made items
- Food shack open weekends

**Tickets:**

- Ages 3 and up require a ticket
- Online tickets recommended for weekends
- Walk-ins welcome
- Dogs are not allowed
- Pumpkins cost extra and are priced by size

**Tip:** Bring your own wagon or canvas bag to carry pumpkins from the field.

**Drive time from Vancouver:** About 45 minutes via Highway 1 east.

**Official site:** [aldoracresfamilyfarm.ca](https://aldoracresfamilyfarm.ca/)

## Abbotsford: Maan Farms Fall at the Farm

Maan Farms runs one of the largest fall festivals in the Fraser Valley. The 2026 season runs Sept 12 to Nov 1.

**Address:** 790 McKenzie Road, Abbotsford

**Hours:** 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.

**Highlights:**

- Expanded five-acre forest pumpkin patch with 15 photo opportunities
- Two corn mazes
- Barnyard Adventureland with corn play pit, zipline, jumping pillows, pedal karts, and bubble hut
- Farm animals including goats, pigs, alpacas, donkeys, Highland cows, mini horses, and sheep
- Pumpkin house (billed as Canada's biggest)
- Seasonal treats like pumpkin spice cream and apple pie samosas

**Tickets:**

- Required daily for ages 3 and up
- Ages 2 and under: Free
- No refunds or rescheduling
- Multiple ticket tiers (General Admission, Pick and Play, Do It All, or membership)
- Pumpkins are priced by size, not weight

**What to bring:** Boots, jackets, water bottles, umbrellas. The farm is open rain or shine. It gets muddy in the corn mazes. Outside food is not allowed. Strollers are permitted but not recommended due to uneven terrain.

**Separate event:** Maan Farms also runs a Halloween Fright Festival starting Oct 2. That requires a different ticket.

**Drive time from Vancouver:** About 65 minutes via Highway 1 east.

**Official site:** [maanfarms.com/pages/fall-at-the-farm](https://maanfarms.com/pages/fall-at-the-farm)

## Abbotsford: Taves Family Farms

Taves Family Farms (also known as the Applebarn) combines apple picking with pumpkin patches and corn mazes.

**Address:** 333 Gladwin Road, Abbotsford

**Magic of Fall dates:** Sept 12 to Oct 31, 2026

**Pumpkin patch opens:** Sept 20, 2026

**Ticket structure:**

- **Weekdays (not holidays or pro-d days):** Free entry to the pumpkin patch. You only pay for pumpkins.
- **Weekends (starting Sept 26):** Admission required. Online tickets save $3 compared to gate prices.
- **Full Magic of Fall experience:** Separate ticket with access to all farm attractions.

**What to see:**

- Magical Pumpkin Garden with storybook details, princess carriage, and heirloom pumpkins
- Apple orchards (Taves has 50 acres of fruit)
- Three corn mazes (Merlin's Maze, Jack O's Maze, Mystery Maze)
- Petting barn with goats, bunnies, pigs, sheep, chickens, ducks, and turkeys
- Mystical Meadow play area with slide, jumping pillow, and pedal karts

**Drive time from Vancouver:** About 70 minutes via Highway 1 east.

**Official site:** [tavesfamilyfarms.com/magic-of-fall-experience/](https://www.tavesfamilyfarms.com/magic-of-fall-experience/)

## Chilliwack: Greendale Acres

Greendale Acres is home to Canada's original corn maze, which has run for more than 26 years.

**Address:** 41905 Yale Road West, Chilliwack

**Corn maze dates:** July 29 to Oct 31, 2026

**Last day of 2026 season:** Nov 1

**What is included with admission:**

- GOT CORN? corn maze (about 12 acres)
- Pumpkin patch
- Farm train ride (Greendale Express)
- Adventure course and barn activities

**Ticket prices (October 2026):**

- Monday to Friday: $17 online, $20 at door
- Saturday and Sunday: $22 online, $25 at door
- Ages 2 and under: Free
- Ages 75 and up: Free

**Pre-buy for October weekends.** The farm recommends booking online ahead of busy Saturdays and Sundays.

**Special events:**

- Pumpkin Patch Adventure, Princess and Heroes Edition: Oct 3, 2026 (Cinderella in morning, Spider-Man in afternoon)
- Giant Pumpkin Weigh-Off: Oct 17, 2026 (weight guessing starts 10 a.m., official weigh-off at 2 p.m.)

**What to bring:** Boots (it gets muddy after rain), jacket, umbrella. The farm is open rain or shine.

**Drive time from Vancouver:** About 90 minutes via Highway 1 east.

**Official site:** [greendaleacres.ca/pages/plan-your-visit](https://www.greendaleacres.ca/pages/plan-your-visit)

## How to plan your day trip

**Check before you go.** Hours can shift. Confirm on the official farm website or social media the day before your visit.

**Book ahead when required.** Maan Farms, Aldor Acres, and Greendale Acres recommend online tickets for weekends. Some farms sell out popular time slots.

**Arrive early.** Weekend crowds build by mid-morning. If you want shorter lines for corn mazes and photo spots, aim to arrive at opening.

**Dress for mud.** Boots or waterproof shoes make a difference. Trails and corn maze paths get messy after rain. Layers help with morning chill and afternoon warmth.

**Bring cash and a card.** Most farms accept tap and debit, but some vendors may be cash-only. Pumpkins are usually priced separately from admission.

**Bring a wagon or bag.** Some farms provide wheelbarrows, but your own wagon or large bag makes it easier to haul pumpkins back to your car.

**Budget for extras.** Entry fees cover the experience, but pumpkins, food, and souvenirs add up. Expect to spend $30 to $60 per person for a full day out, depending on the farm.
