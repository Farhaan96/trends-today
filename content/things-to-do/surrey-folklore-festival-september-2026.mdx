The 7th Annual Folklore Festival brings a week of cultural performances, dance workshops, and visual arts to Surrey starting this week.

## Free dance workshops run Sept. 2-3 with no registration required. RSVP for the Sept. 5 artist session. Gala tickets are $15-25.

Folk Star Arts Academy Society presents the festival across multiple Surrey venues. The [City of Surrey event page](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/folklore-festival) lists the full schedule.

---

## Festival schedule at a glance

The main festival events run Sept. 2-6 at three venues:

- **Sept. 2 and 3, 6-8 p.m.**: Multicultural Folk Dance Workshops at Guru Angad Dev School, 13479 77 Avenue. Free. No registration required.
- **Sept. 5, 5-7 p.m.**: Meet the Artists and Cultural Knowledge Sharing at Clayton Community Centre, 7155 187A Street. Free with RSVP.
- **Sept. 6, 4:30-8:30 p.m.**: Folklore Festival Multicultural Gala at Bell Performing Arts Centre, 6250 144 Street. Tickets **$15-25**.

---

## Month-long exhibition continues through Sept. 27

The Punjabi Cultural Heritage and Calligraphy Exhibition opened Sept. 1 and runs through **Sept. 27** at Newton Cultural Centre. Admission is free.

The city page describes the festival as a celebration of cultural diversity, heritage, music, dance, and visual arts. The gala features professional artists and community performers from diverse cultural backgrounds.

---

## What to know before going

The dance workshops and artist session are free. Workshops do not require advance registration, but the Meet the Artists session on Sept. 5 needs an RSVP.

Gala tickets range from **$15 to $25**. The city page does not list a ticket link, so check the Folk Star Arts Academy Society channels or the event page closer to Sept. 6 for purchase details.
