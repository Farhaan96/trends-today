Crave Halal Fest returns to Surrey this weekend with a two-day community celebration at **Surrey Civic Plaza**.

## The festival runs Sept. 5 and 6 at 13450 104 Ave, Surrey. Admission is free. Check the event website for hours.

The [City of Surrey event page](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/crave-halal-fest) lists the festival as a food, shopping, and cultural event hosted by the community.

---

## What to expect

The city describes the festival as featuring:

- **Halal food vendors**: A range of halal cuisine options
- **Local businesses and retailers**: Shopping from community vendors
- **Artisans**: Handmade goods and crafts
- **Community organizations**: Local groups and services
- **Live entertainment**: Performances throughout the event
- **Family-friendly activities**: Programming for all ages

---

## Venue and admission

The festival takes place at **Surrey Civic Plaza**, located at **13450 104 Ave, Surrey, BC**.

Admission is **free**.

The city listing does not post event hours. Visitors should check the [Marhaba Events website](https://marhabaevents.ca/) closer to the date for the full schedule and timing.
