**Stanley Park is bigger than New York's Central Park. Vancouver's park covers 400 hectares. Central Park is 843 acres, which works out to about 341 hectares.**

That makes Stanley Park about 17% bigger (our math: 400 divided by 341). The City of Vancouver calls it the city's "first, largest, and most beloved urban park," a 400 hectare natural West Coast rainforest with trails, beaches, wildlife and Canada's largest aquarium.

## The size, side by side

- **Stanley Park:** 400 hectares, per the [City of Vancouver](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park.aspx).
- **Central Park:** 843 acres, per the [Central Park Conservancy](https://www.centralparknyc.org/about). At 0.404686 hectares per acre, that is about 341 hectares (our math).

## The seawall took 63 years

Construction of the seawall began in Stanley Park in **1917**. The entire loop around the park was declared officially complete on September 21, **1980**, with the final paving between Third Beach and Second Beach, according to the [City's seawall page](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/seawall.aspx). That is 63 years from start to finish (our math).

Since then the seawall has kept growing. The City says the **28 km** seaside greenway, from the Vancouver Convention Centre to Spanish Banks Park, is "the world's longest uninterrupted waterfront path."

## How long the loop takes

The City says the **9 km** stretch around Stanley Park takes **2 to 3 hours to walk** or **about 1 hour to cycle**. Walkers and joggers use the side closest to the water; cyclists and inline skaters use the inside path. The park is open from 6am to 10pm unless otherwise posted.

## Heads up: a closure until Nov 14

The seawall between **Lumberman's Arch and Third Beach is fully closed until November 14, 2026**, while the Park Board removes speed gates and does its annual rock scaling, the City says on its [seawall upgrades page](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/stanley-park-seawall-safety-and-accessibility-upgrades.aspx). People on foot and on bikes can follow detour signs through Beaver Lake. Intermittent closures may continue until November 20, 2026, so follow posted signs.
