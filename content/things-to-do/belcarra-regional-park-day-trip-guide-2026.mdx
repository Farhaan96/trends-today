**Can you do a Belcarra Regional Park day trip from Vancouver without a car?** Yes. Use [TransLink Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/) for your door-to-park trip. Ride SkyTrain to **Moody Centre Station**, then **#182** (**Moody Centre Station / Belcarra**). Alight near **White Pine Beach** on Sasamat Lake or continue toward the **Belcarra Picnic Area** on Belcarra Bay Road. Walk lake loops, swamp boardwalks, or inlet trails. Prefer the bus on sunny weekends when park lots fill. Confirm Trip Planner times and **posted park hours** at the entrance before you leave.

## Why Belcarra works as a half day

Belcarra packs lake swimming, forest loops, and Burrard Inlet / Indian Arm views into one regional park. You can ride SkyTrain to Moody Centre, transfer to the Belcarra bus, and walk without gambling on a scarce stall.

That is the point of this planner: one park, clear transit, trail and dog-sign honesty.

If you liked the Port Moody waterfront of [Rocky Point and Shoreline](/things-to-do/port-moody-day-trip-guide-2026) or the Point Grey forest of [Pacific Spirit](/things-to-do/pacific-spirit-regional-park-day-trip-guide-2026), this is the Tri-Cities lake and inlet sibling. For a North Shore waterfront contrast later, pair with [Ambleside](/things-to-do/ambleside-park-west-vancouver-day-trip-guide-2026).

## How to get there without a car

### Moody Centre Station, then #182 (preferred)

[TransLink lists route **182** as Moody Centre Station / Belcarra](https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/182/direction/0/schedule). [TransitDB route 182](http://www.transitdb.ca/route/182/) maps weekday, Saturday, and Sunday service, with inbound headsign **182 Belcarra** and outbound **182 Moody Centre Station**.

Typical park-area stops from public TransitDB listings:

- **Westbound Bedwell Bay Rd @ White Pine Beach Rd** (stop **60271**) for Sasamat Lake / White Pine Beach access
- **Southbound Belcarra Bay Rd @ Salish Rd** (stop **58758**) near the Belcarra picnic corridor
- Terminus area toward **Belcarra Bay Rd @ Whiskey Cove Lane** (stop **58960**)

Use [TransLink Trip Planner](https://www.translink.ca/) for the return trip to Moody Centre Station. Treat door-to-park times as **typical**, not guaranteed. Build a buffer for the transfer and the last walk to your trailhead.

### Summer service note (confirm live)

The [Village of Belcarra transit page](https://belcarra.ca/community/transit/) summarized TransLink summer 2026 changes starting **June 8, 2026**: more weekend and holiday trips on **#182** until September, plus a seasonal weekend/holiday extension of **#181** to Belcarra until September. Those boost windows were time-limited. On **Sept. 30, 2026**, do **not** assume summer weekend frequencies. Confirm the live Trip Planner trip and headsign the morning you go.

### Bike access

Cycling is permitted on **Springboard Trail** per the brochure. **Sugar Mountain**, **Saddle Ridge**, and **Bear Claw** trails are open to cyclists but are rugged with steep sections. Cyclists yield to pedestrians. Stay on designated trails.

## Driving and parking (if you skip transit)

Prefer the bus so the half day is not a parking hunt. Metro Vancouver's brochure says sunny summer days fill lots quickly at **White Pine Beach** and the **Belcarra Picnic Area**. Seasonal pay parking was introduced in **2021**. Respect parking restrictions on adjacent municipal streets. There is **no parking on Bedwell Bay Road**.

This guide does **not** invent hourly pay-parking dollar amounts. Confirm live rates and lot rules on site or on metrovancouver.org before you go.

Park gates lock when the park is closed. Do not leave a car inside a gated lot past posted hours. Village of Belcarra notes Metro Vancouver's after-hours number **604-432-9483** for cars locked behind the park gate.

## Hour-by-hour half-day outline

1. **Morning transit.** SkyTrain to **Moody Centre Station**. Transfer to **#182** toward Belcarra.
2. **Sasamat Lake block (60-90 minutes).** Alight near **White Pine Beach Rd**. Walk the **Sasamat Lake Loop** (about **3.2 km** return) or shorter shoreline sections. Use the **Beach By-Pass** if you are with a dog and need to avoid White Pine Beach.
3. **Optional Belcarra Picnic Area block (45-75 minutes).** Continue on **#182** or walk toward Belcarra Bay. Washrooms, picnic tables, wharf, and floats. Easy **Bedwell Bay Trail** (about **1.2 km** return) to tidal flats.
4. **Optional Woodhaven Swamp (30-45 minutes).** Gently sloped **1.2 km** loop around a drowned forest wetland. Watch for wildlife. Stay on the trail.
5. **Return bus.** Ride **#182** back to Moody Centre with buffer before evening plans. Confirm Trip Planner.

Swap lake-first versus picnic-first based on your alight stop and weather.

## What to expect on the trails

Metro Vancouver's Dec. 2022 brochure describes intertidal life at Belcarra beach, forest glades, Woodhaven Swamp, and swimming at Sasamat Lake.

**Easy** brochure examples:

- **Bedwell Bay Trail:** about **1.2 km** return, roughly **20 minutes**
- **Woodhaven Swamp Loop:** about **1.2 km** return, roughly **30 minutes**
- **Sasamat Lake Loop:** about **3.2 km** return, roughly **1 hour**
- **Springboard Trail:** about **8 km** return, roughly **2 hours** (hardened path for cyclists and pedestrians)

**Intermediate** brochure examples:

- **Admiralty Point Trail:** about **6 km** return (Admiralty Point about **1.5 hours**, Burns Point about **2 hours**)
- **Jug Island Beach Trail:** about **6 km** return, roughly **2 hours**, steep sections to a pocket beach
- **Woodhaven Trail:** about **4.2 km** return, roughly **1.5 hours**

**Challenging** brochure examples climb toward **Buntzen Ridge Viewpoint** via Buntzen Ridge or Sugar Mountain trails. Stay on designated trails.

Rules called out in the brochure: no liquor, no cannabis, no camping, no vegetation removal, no shellfish harvesting in Burrard Inlet and Indian Arm. Smoking is not permitted except in designated areas marked by signs. Swim at your own risk. There are **no lifeguards** at park beaches, including Sasamat Lake. Fires are only permitted in the designated fire ring at Belcarra Picnic Area shelters, and restrictions may apply in hot weather. Confirm live fire danger and park notices before you go.

Area office for təmtəmíxʷtən / Belcarra: **604-520-6442**. Park conditions: metrovancouver.org. Emergencies: **9-1-1**.

## Dogs, beaches, and signs

Dogs are welcome but **must be leashed**. Scoop waste into dog waste bins.

Brochure prohibitions:

- **White Pine Beach**
- Swimming and fishing decks of the **Floatwalk**
- Most pocket beaches along **Sasamat Lake**

To enjoy the Sasamat Lake Loop and avoid White Pine Beach, follow the **Beach By-Pass** route. Do not invent a blanket off-leash park. Read the sign at each junction.

## Food and cafes

This guide does **not** quote village cafe hours. Pack water and a snack. Picnic tables sit at White Pine Beach and the Belcarra Picnic Area. Shelter bookings: **604-432-6352** per brochure. If you want food after the walk in Port Moody or Belcarra Village, confirm operator hours the morning you go.

## Weather, crowds, and honesty

Belcarra and White Pine Beach see heavy use on warm weekends. Arrive early by bus, or pick a shoulder day. Fall is strong for cooler forest walks, but mud and early dusk matter. Plan to be at your return stop with bus buffer before you are tired and cold. Earliest park closing is **5 p.m.** Many days close later, but only the posted entrance hours count.

Bears and cougars live in this landscape. Stay calm and back away if you encounter wildlife. Do not leave garbage or unattended food.
