TAIWANfest returns to downtown Vancouver this Labour Day weekend with a three-day cultural celebration under the theme "Islands in the Wind."

## The festival runs Sept. 5 to 7 at multiple downtown venues. Admission is free. Check the official website for the full schedule.

The [official TAIWANfest website](https://vancouvertaiwanfest.ca/) lists programming connecting Taiwan, Canada, and the world, with a Scotland cultural exchange theme this year.

---

## What to expect

The festival features:

- **Live music**: Performances at outdoor and indoor venues
- **Artist market**: Local and international vendors
- **Exhibitions**: Cultural displays and installations
- **Workshops**: Hands-on programming
- **Culinary programs**: Food and cultural experiences

---

## Venues and hours

TAIWANfest programming takes place at multiple locations:

- **šxʷƛ̓ənəq Xwtl'e7énk Square** (north of Vancouver Art Gallery): Sat-Sun Sept. 5-6, noon to 10 p.m.; Mon Sept. 7, noon to 6 p.m.
- **Granville Street**: Noon to 6 p.m. all three days
- **Vancouver Playhouse**
- **ANNEX**
- **Vancouver Public Library Central Branch**

Admission is **free**.

Visitors should confirm the full schedule and venue-specific hours on the [official TAIWANfest website](https://vancouvertaiwanfest.ca/).
