Surrey's free Clayton Coffee House runs Friday, July 24, from **6 to 7:30 p.m.** at Clayton Community Centre, with all-ages drop-in admission and limited capacity.

[The City of Surrey lists](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/clayton-coffee-house) the event for the Performing Arts Studio at **7155 187A Street**. The city describes it as a monthly performance night where residents can watch local artists take the stage.

## What the event is

Clayton Coffee House is built around short community performances. Surrey says the format can include poets, spoken-word artists, novelists, musicians, improvisers, comedians, and other performers.

Admission is free. The capacity note is the useful planning detail: this is a drop-in event, but Surrey says capacity is limited, so arriving right at the start may be riskier than arriving a little early.

## Performer format

The city says performers are given **5 to 8 minutes**, with limited drop-in spots available on show day. That means the night is designed for a rotating lineup rather than one long set.

Surrey's broader summer community-art update also lists Clayton Coffee House as part of its seasonal arts lineup. The event is the near-term option because it lands Friday night, while other listed items run on different dates or require separate registration.
