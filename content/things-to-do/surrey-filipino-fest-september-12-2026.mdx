**Filipino Fest** is back at **Museum of Surrey** on **Saturday, September 12, 2026**, from **11 a.m. to 4 p.m.** Admission is **free**. If you want a one-day cultural stop with food, performances, and kids activities in Cloverdale, this is the clear Surrey pick for the weekend.

## The short version

The City of Surrey lists Filipino Fest at **Museum of Surrey, 17710 56A Avenue**, on **Saturday, Sept. 12**, **11 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, with **free** tickets. Expect Filipino food vendors and food trucks, live performances, textile displays, Tagalog and English storytelling, market vendors, family art, and a **sensory-friendly space** on site. Confirm the day-of details on the [Filipino Fest event page](https://www.surrey.ca/arts-culture/museum-of-surrey/whats-on/filipino-fest) or [surrey.ca/museum](https://www.surrey.ca/museum) before you leave.

## What you can do there

According to the City of Surrey news release (August 13, 2026) and the museum event listing:

- **Food:** Vendors named by the city include **Cake It Easy**, **Nanay Kitchen**, **Dale's Food Products**, **Kasalo**, and **Timplado Filipino Street Food Truck**.
- **Stage and culture:** Performances listed include **Glisha**, **The Kuyas**, **Raffy Swap One**, **Red Filos**, **Mabuhay Arnis**, and **Grupo Ilocano Association of BC**, plus cultural displays, textile exhibits, and photo opportunities.
- **Families:** Children's activities and storytelling sessions (Tagalog and English) are part of the program.
- **Quiet option:** A sensory-friendly space is provided for anyone who needs a calmer break during the day.

Treat performer and vendor names as planning context. Lineups can shift; use the official Surrey pages as the last word.
