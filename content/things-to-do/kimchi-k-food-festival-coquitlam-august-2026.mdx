The Kimchi and K-Food Festival returns to Coquitlam Town Centre Park this weekend with two days of Korean food, live performances, and cultural experiences.

## Festival runs August 28-29 with free admission and main stage performances from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.

The festival describes itself as Western Canada's largest Korean food and cultural festival. According to the official schedule, both days run from **11 a.m. to 8 p.m.** at Coquitlam Town Centre Park.

Admission is free.

---

## Friday schedule highlights

The Friday main stage opens at 11 a.m. with performances scheduled throughout the day.

Key Friday events according to the official schedule:

- **11:35 a.m.**: Yong In University Taekwondo Special Performance
- **12:10 p.m.**: Studio KJD Performance
- **12:45 p.m.**: Gongsaemi Family Samulnori Performance
- **3 p.m.**: Korean Traditional Fusion Music by K-Sonic Collectives
- **5:05 p.m.**: K-Pop Dance Championship first half
- **7:10 p.m.**: K-Pop Dance Championship finals

The festival says K-pop singer G.NA will appear as Special MC for the K-Pop Dance Championship starting at 5 p.m.

---

## Saturday schedule highlights

Saturday features a live kimchi-making demonstration and a youth scholarship awards ceremony.

Key Saturday events according to the official schedule:

- **11:10 a.m.**: Nanta Performance
- **11:35 a.m.**: Korean Traditional Samulnori Performance
- **12:05 p.m.**: Live Kimchi-Making Demonstration
- **12:40 p.m.**: Yong In University Taekwondo Special Performance
- **2 p.m.**: Kyung Hee University Taekwondo Demonstration Team Special Performance
- **3 p.m.**: Youth Scholarship Awards Ceremony
- **4 p.m.**: CIDC Championship first half
- **7:10 p.m.**: CIDC Championship finals

---

## What else to expect

The festival website lists hanbok experiences, K-beauty showcases, and Korean street food vendors. The festival is presented with support from Tourism Coquitlam, the Korean Consulate General in Vancouver, and Korea Tourism Organization.

Festival details:

- **Location**: Coquitlam Town Centre Park
- **Dates**: Friday, August 28 and Saturday, August 29, 2026
- **Hours**: 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. both days
- **Admission**: Free
