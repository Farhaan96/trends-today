Vancouver has a free fireworks night to plan around before the BC Day long weekend. [The City of Vancouver says](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/summer-lights-in-english-bay.aspx) **Summer Lights in English Bay** takes place Friday, **July 31, 2026**, at English Bay Beach Park, with no tickets required.

The city says the one-night event falls during Vancouver Pride's closing weekend. It also warns that large crowds, road closures, and traffic delays are expected around English Bay, the West End, and downtown.

## Where and what is confirmed

The city's event page gives enough fixed details for residents to decide whether to go.

- **Event**: Summer Lights in English Bay
- **Date**: Friday, July 31, 2026
- **Location**: English Bay Beach Park
- **Admission**: Free, with no tickets or registration required
- **On site**: Food trucks are planned

[The city's June announcement](https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/announcing-summer-lights-in-english-bay-june-2026.aspx) says the event was created after the Celebration of Light would not proceed in 2026. It says the new event is presented by the City of Vancouver and produced by BrandLive, with support from the Vancouver Park Board.

## How to get there

The city is telling people to walk, bike, roll, take transit, use Mobi, or ride a Lime scooter whenever possible. It says bus routes may be rerouted or delayed, so anyone relying on transit should check [TransLink alerts](https://www.translink.ca/alerts) before leaving.

Parking will be tight. The city says on-street parking is limited, no-parking signs should be watched closely, and parking lots will be closed at Kits Beach, Hadden Park, and Vanier Park.

Accessible parking is expected to open at **5 pm** on a first-come, first-served basis for vehicles with a valid accessible parking permit.

## What to leave at home

The city's visitor rules are practical, especially for anyone bringing kids or watching from the water.

- **Drones**: Strictly prohibited because of airspace restrictions and event safety rules
- **Smoking, glass, and alcohol**: Not permitted at English Bay
- **Barbecues**: The city says to avoid using them
- **Pets**: The city says fireworks and large crowds can be frightening, so pets should stay home

Boaters are being told to carry the required licence and equipment, avoid alcohol while operating a vessel, and stay outside the English Bay exclusion zone marked by marine buoys.

## Why it matters for July plans

The timing matters because it is a free waterfront event on a long-weekend Friday, and the city is already warning about movement around the West End and nearby beaches.
