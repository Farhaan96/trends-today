[The City of Burnaby lists](https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/events/walk-and-chat-mayor) the next **Walk and Chat with the Mayor** for Sunday, **July 26**, giving residents a low-cost morning plan before the last week of July.

The walk runs from **9 to 10 am** and meets at **Christine Sinclair Community Centre**, 3713 Kensington Avenue. Burnaby says the monthly walks run rain or shine.

## What is confirmed

The official listing is simple, but it has enough planning detail for anyone choosing a Sunday morning outing.

- **Date**: Sunday, July 26, 2026
- **Time**: 9 to 10 am
- **Meeting point**: Christine Sinclair Community Centre
- **Address**: 3713 Kensington Avenue
- **Afterward**: $2 pancake breakfast at 10 am

Burnaby says the pancake breakfast is served by the Burnaby Firefighters Charitable Society.

## Who it fits

This is a practical fit for residents who want a short community walk, a chance to talk with the mayor in an informal setting, or a low-cost morning stop near Kensington Avenue.

The event is also useful for anyone trying to keep weekend plans flexible. A one-hour start-to-finish walk is easier to plan around than a full festival or ticketed outing.
