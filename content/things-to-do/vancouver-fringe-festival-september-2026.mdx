The **42nd Vancouver Fringe Festival** is on now: **Thursday, September 10 through Sunday, September 20, 2026**, centred on **Granville Island**. If you want theatre, comedy, dance, drag, or music in short bursts rather than one big night, this is the Lower Mainland window.

## The short version

Granville Island’s festival listing describes **84 shows**, hundreds of performances, and tickets **from about $16** (often in a **$16 to $25** range depending on the show). The free **Fringe Patio** music series runs through Sept. 20. **Kids Fringe** is free on **Saturday and Sunday, Sept. 12 and 13**, from **10 a.m. to 2 p.m.** Buy tickets online at [vancouverfringe.com](https://www.vancouverfringe.com/) or at **Festival House, 1398 Cartwright Street**. Tickets are **not** sold at performance venues. Aim to arrive **at least 15 minutes early**. Confirm exact show prices and start times on the festival site before you leave home.

## What Fringe is (in one sentence)

Fringe festivals are largely uncurated: artists enter by lottery rather than a presenter picking a lineup, which is why the program feels wide and a little chaotic in a good way. Vancouver’s edition has been running since 1985.

## Dates, free days, and what it costs

**Festival window:** Sept. 10 to 20, 2026.

**Paid shows:** Artists set their own ticket prices. Granville Island currently lists a typical range of **$16 to $25**. The Fringe says it returns **100% of the base ticket price** to artists. Fees and exact totals vary by show, so treat the range as a planning guide and check each listing.

**Free options that still feel like Fringe:**

- **Kids Fringe** (Sept. 12 and 13, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.): free programming for kids and grown-ups. Details: [Kids Fringe 2026](https://www.vancouverfringe.com/kids-fringe-2026/).
- **Fringe Patio** music: free, all-ages patio programming listed through Sept. 20 (it started over Labour Day weekend).

**Accessibility notes** (from the Granville Island listing): venues publish Accessibility Guides. Some shows offer ASL, visual guides, or VocalEye described performances (VocalEye dates listed for Sept. 19 to 20). Use the site filters for content warnings and age guidance.

## How to buy tickets (and where not to)

According to the festival’s How to Fringe guide:

- **Online:** [vancouverfringe.com/events](https://www.vancouverfringe.com/events/)
- **Phone:** 604-336-1736 (hours vary; confirm on the site)
- **In person during the festival:** Festival House, **1398 Cartwright Street**, Granville Island. The guide lists weekday box office hours **4 p.m. to 10 p.m.** and Saturday/Sunday **10 a.m. to 10 p.m.** Other Granville Island box offices may also be open; check the day-of listing.
- **Not at venues:** do not expect to buy at the performance door.

If you are booking **six or more** shows, look at a **MultiBuy** pass on the festival site. **Fringe+** is an optional add-on for early-access and discount perks if you want more than a single-ticket night.

Latecomers may be locked out. Build a buffer between shows so you are not sprinting across the island.

## Getting to Granville Island

Most Fringe stages sit on Granville Island. Practical arrival options the festival highlights:

- **Transit:** The **#50** bus serves the Granville Island entrance from Waterfront Station (via downtown Granville Street) or from Olympic Village Station. Use TransLink’s trip planner for the exact trip.
- **Ferry:** False Creek Ferries run frequently with stops that include Sunset Beach, David Lam Park, and Yaletown. Check ferry hours for evening shows.
- **Bike:** Free bike parking behind Granville Island Brewing is listed with daytime hours and later Friday to Sunday close times.
- **Car:** Paid parking on and near the island is limited. For evening stacks of shows, transit or ferry is usually less stressful.

Festival House at **1398 Cartwright Street** is the practical hub for tickets and orientation once you are on the island.

## A simple first-timer plan

1. Pick **two or three** shows on [the events page](https://www.vancouverfringe.com/events/), not eight. Leave gaps for walking and food.
2. Add one **free** beat: Patio music on a weeknight, or Kids Fringe if you have younger kids on the weekend.
3. Buy tickets **before** you arrive. Screenshot your confirmations.
4. Eat at the Public Market or a nearby counter between shows so you are not choosing dinner at 9:50 p.m. with a 10 p.m. curtain.
5. Check content warnings and accessibility notes for each title.
