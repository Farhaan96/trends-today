Vancouver Whitecaps host LAFC at BC Place on **Saturday, Aug. 1**, with kickoff at **4:30 p.m. Pacific**.

The team's fresh [spectator information page](https://www.whitecapsfc.com/news/spectator-aug-1-2026-pride-presented-telus) says public gates open at **3 p.m.** and asks guests to arrive early because electronic turnstiles are in use.

This guide replaces the route's earlier attendance-focused framing with the confirmed details a person needs before leaving for the stadium. It excludes promotional activations and other material that does not help with entry.

## Match time and entry sequence

The [BC Place event page](https://www.bcplace.com/?event=vancouver-whitecaps-fc-vs-lafc) independently confirms the date, kickoff and gate schedule stated above.

The venue page also says there is no in-and-out access after entry.

Before reaching the turnstile, guests can prepare in this order:

- Add the mobile ticket to a phone wallet before arriving.
- Increase screen brightness and have the barcode ready before the scan point.
- Meet the rest of the group before entering because re-entry is not permitted.
- Use the ticket office at **Gate B** if ticket help is needed.

The Whitecaps page recommends early arrival. That advice is tied to the electronic entry process, not a prediction about a particular wait time.

## Bag limits and payment at BC Place

BC Place recommends bringing no bag when possible. Its event guidance and the Whitecaps page list two permitted maximums:

- A clear bag must be smaller than **12 by 12 by 6 inches**.
- One small opaque bag can be no larger than **6.5 by 8.5 inches**.
- Medical and accessibility needs should be checked against the venue's current official guidance before travel.

The venue is cashless. The spectator page lists a cash-to-card kiosk at **Section 235**, giving guests who arrive with cash an official conversion option inside the building.

Anyone carrying more than the stated limits should review the current venue page before leaving home. This article does not extend or interpret the bag policy beyond the dimensions published by the venue and club.

## Stadium-district access and supporter sections

BC Place says parking and street access may be limited because several events are happening in the stadium district. That is the venue's own access notice, not a claim that every route will be delayed.

The practical checks are:

- Review the current BC Place event page before departure.
- Leave enough time to reach the gate after it opens at 3 p.m.
- Keep the mobile ticket available until entry is complete.
- Follow venue directions around any street or parking restriction in effect that day.

The Whitecaps page also identifies sections **201 to 207** and **248 to 254** as home-supporter areas. It says LAFC colours and visiting-team support are not permitted in those sections, and affected guests may be relocated.

That section rule matters when accepting or transferring a ticket. The safest check is the section number shown in the mobile ticket before travelling to BC Place.

## What to verify on match day

The core schedule is fixed across the two material official pages, while operational guidance can still be refreshed. The [official Whitecaps schedule](https://www.whitecapsfc.com/schedule/matches) remains a useful final check for the club's live match route.

Before leaving, confirm these items:

- The mobile ticket is in the correct phone wallet.
- The selected bag remains within one of the published size limits.
- The ticket section is appropriate for the person using it.
- The current venue page has not added a new operational instruction.

The matchday reader job is narrow: know the time, prepare entry and avoid bringing an item the venue does not permit. The confirmed sequence is the public gate opening followed by kickoff.
