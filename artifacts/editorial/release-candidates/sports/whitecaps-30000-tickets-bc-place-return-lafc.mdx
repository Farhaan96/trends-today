The [BC Place event page](https://www.bcplace.com/?event=vancouver-whitecaps-fc-vs-lafc) says public gates open at **3 p.m.** and recommends early arrival for the electronic entry process.

The practical job is to prepare the issued ticket format, bring a permitted bag and understand what happens at the turnstile before reaching the stadium.

## Match time and entry sequence

The venue page also says there is no in-and-out access after entry.

That rule matters for groups making separate travel plans. Choose an outside meeting point before anyone scans a ticket, and bring everything needed for the match through the first entry because leaving the building ends access under the current event policy.

BC Place gives different turnstile instructions by ticket format:

- **Mobile Wallet**: Tap the phone near the contactless reader at the top of the turnstile screen.
- **Ticketmaster app or physical ticket**: Scan the barcode at the reader below the turnstile screen.
- **Several tickets on one phone**: Wait for the green light, let one person enter, then move to the next ticket.
- **Any ticket format**: Meet the group before entry because re-entry is not permitted.

At security screening, BC Place asks guests to keep personal items, including a permitted bag, on their person while walking through the detection system and toward the turnstiles. That makes it useful to organize the ticket and bag before joining the entry line.

## Bag limits and payment at BC Place

BC Place recommends bringing no bag when possible. Its event guidance lists two permitted maximums:

- A clear bag can be no larger than **12 by 12 by 6 inches**.
- One small opaque bag can be no larger than **6.5 by 8.5 inches**.
- Medical and accessibility needs should be checked against the venue's current official guidance before travel.

The venue is cashless. Guests should prepare a payment card before entry and use current venue directions for any available cash-to-card service.

The venue page also carries accessibility information. Guests who need those services can check the current guidance before travel.

Anyone carrying more than the stated limits should choose an item that fits the published policy before leaving home. The two permitted dimensions apply to different bag types, so a small opaque bag does not use the larger clear-bag maximum.
