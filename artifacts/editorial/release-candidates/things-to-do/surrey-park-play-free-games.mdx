[The City of Surrey's T.E. Scott Park listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/park-play-te-scott-park) and [Robertson Drive Park listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/park-play-robertson-drive-park) say **Park Play** continues this week with free outdoor games at two Surrey parks.

The listings give families two weekday options for borrowing a park play kit, joining a staff-led group game, playing a lawn game, or trying a seek and find activity.

## Dates and locations

The next listed Park Play sessions are:

- **T.E. Scott Park, 7014 148 St**: Monday, July 27, 2026, from **4 to 6 p.m.**
- **Robertson Drive Park, 12714 92 Ave**: Wednesday, July 29, 2026, from **2 to 4 p.m.**

The City lists tickets and pricing as **free** for both pages.

For recurrence, one listing says the program runs Mondays until August 17, excluding August 3. The other says the program runs Wednesdays until August 19.

## What the City says is offered

Both City pages describe the same basic setup. Visitors can borrow a park play kit, play a lawn game, join a staff-led group game, or try a seek and find activity.

The useful difference is timing. The T.E. Scott Park session is late afternoon on Monday, while the Robertson Drive Park session is midafternoon on Wednesday.

The pages do not state a capacity limit or weather plan. They use the same Park Play description and list the same City contact phone number.

Both listings frame the sessions as summer park programs and point readers to more Park Play information.

## Who to contact

For event questions, both pages list **604-501-5050**.

Use the T.E. Scott Park page for the Monday session and the Robertson Drive Park page for the Wednesday session, because each page is the official listing for that park's dates and details.
