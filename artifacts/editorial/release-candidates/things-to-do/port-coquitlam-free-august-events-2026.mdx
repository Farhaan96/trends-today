[The City of Port Coquitlam's Cinema Under the Stars page](https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/explore-poco/events/cinema-under-stars) and [Music in the Square page](https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/explore-poco/events/music-square) list free August options for families who want an outdoor movie night or a Sunday afternoon concert.

The useful decision is simple: pick a late movie night at Gates Park or Evergreen Park, or choose an afternoon concert at Leigh Square. Both series are free, but they need different planning.

## The quick August schedule

The remaining city-listed dates start August 8:

- **August 8**: Cinema Under the Stars shows *Pirates of the Caribbean: Curse of the Black Pearl* at Gates Park Field 2.
- **August 9**: Music in the Square runs at Leigh Square from 2 to 5 p.m.
- **August 22**: Cinema Under the Stars shows *Night at the Museum* at Evergreen Park.
- **August 23**: Music in the Square returns at Leigh Square from 2 to 5 p.m.
- **September 5**: Cinema Under the Stars shows *Zootopia 2* at Gates Park Field 2.

That gives families two August movie nights, two August concert dates, and one early September movie date if August fills up.

## Movie nights need chairs and blankets

[The City's Cinema Under the Stars page](https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/explore-poco/events/cinema-under-stars) says the free outdoor movie series continues at Evergreen Park and Gates Park. It tells visitors to bring blankets and lawn chairs, and says pre-movie activities are part of the setup.

The City says the concession stand will be open at Gates Park, while popcorn will be available for purchase at Evergreen Park. If you are choosing between dates, Gates Park is the option with a listed concession, while Evergreen Park is the option with popcorn noted on the official page.

The practical thing to remember is that the August movie times are listed for **8:45 p.m.** The September 5 movie is listed for **8 p.m.**

That later start makes the movie series better for groups that can stay out after sunset and bring their own seating.

## Music in the Square is a Sunday afternoon option

[The City's Music in the Square page](https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/explore-poco/events/music-square) describes the series as free Sunday afternoon concerts at Leigh Square, with food trucks and professional musicians across genres such as jazz, pop, rock, blues, folk, world fusion, and contemporary music.

The remaining listed dates are:

- **August 9**: Mountain Sound at 2 p.m., Generations at 3 p.m., and Steve Marriott at 4:15 p.m.
- **August 23**: Rebecca Sichon at 2 p.m., The Beer O' Crats at 3 p.m., and Cal Volpe at 4:15 p.m.

Both dates are listed from **2 to 5 p.m.** at **Leigh Square, 2253 Leigh Square Pl**. This is the simpler option if you want an afternoon event rather than a late movie night.

## Which date should you save?

Pick by the kind of outing you actually want:

- **Afternoon concert**: Music in the Square on August 9 or August 23.
- **Movie-night plan**: Cinema Under the Stars on August 8 or August 22.
- **Concession stand noted**: Gates Park on August 8 or September 5.
- **Popcorn noted**: Evergreen Park on August 22.

The practical move is to choose the weekend first, then check the official City page for any schedule updates before leaving, especially for outdoor events.
