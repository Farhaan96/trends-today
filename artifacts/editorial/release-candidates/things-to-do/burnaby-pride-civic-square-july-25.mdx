**Burnaby Pride 2026** is scheduled for Saturday, **July 25**, from **3 to 7 p.m.** at Burnaby Civic Square, giving residents one confirmed Pride event to plan around this weekend.

## The Confirmed Plan

The City of Burnaby lists the event as a community celebration and says it is now in its eighth year. Burnaby Pride's event page describes the festival as free, family-friendly, and built around live performances, community programming, and interactive booths.

## What To Know Before Going

The venue is **Burnaby Civic Square, 6100 Willingdon Avenue**. Burnaby Pride says the afternoon typically includes live music, drag performances, comedy, interactive booths, and community organizations sharing resources and ways to get involved.

That wording matters because the page does not yet list a full act-by-act schedule. Readers who care about a specific performer or activity should check Burnaby Pride before leaving.
