Surrey has a free public-art drop-in on the calendar this week at Newton Athletic Park. The City of Surrey says the Our City placemaking meetup runs **Tuesday, July 28**, from **4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.** at **7395 128 St.**

The event is for people who want a short, hands-on park activity rather than a ticketed show. Surrey's listing asks visitors to find the Our City tent and help make colourful fence art.

## Where and when to go

These are the confirmed planning details from the City of Surrey listing:

- **Date**: Tuesday, July 28, 2026
- **Time**: 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- **Address**: Newton Athletic Park, 7395 128 St.
- **Cost**: Free

The page describes the format as drop-in and does not list a separate registration step.

## What Surrey says the activity includes

Surrey describes the event as a chance to "discover how placemaking can transform shared spaces." The activity is fence art, with the city saying participants can add colour and vibrancy to the community space.

The activity is part of the city's Our City event stream. The page points readers to Surrey's Our City information for other placemaking events, but this specific listing is for the Newton Athletic Park fence-art meetup.

The contact note on the listing also directs readers to learn about Our City through Surrey's event information.
