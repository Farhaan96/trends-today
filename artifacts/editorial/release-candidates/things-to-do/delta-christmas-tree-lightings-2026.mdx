**Delta is holding two free Christmas tree lightings in November 2026. Ladner Village goes first on Thursday, November 26, and North Delta follows on Saturday, November 28. Both events run from 5 to 8 p.m.**

The city says each gathering includes seasonal activities, entertainment, photos with Santa and an artisan market. The North Delta program also has skating and an optional paid movie.

---

## Dates, times and locations

- **Ladner:** Thursday, November 26, from 5 to 8 p.m. at Delta Street and 48th Avenue in Ladner Village. The tree lighting is at **7 p.m.**
- **North Delta:** Saturday, November 28, from 5 to 8 p.m. at North Delta Recreation Centre. The main tree is outside the North Delta Centre for the Arts, and the lighting is at **7 p.m.**
- **Admission:** Both tree-lighting events have free entry.

Delta's announcement says the city is continuing the Ladner Business Association's longstanding lighting tradition. The North Delta event returns to the recreation-centre and arts-centre area.

## What is included at both events

The two programs share several seasonal activities:

- **Photos with Santa:** Offered at both locations.
- **Artisan markets:** Local vendors will sell gifts and stocking stuffers.
- **Family activities:** Crafts, cookie decorating and Christmas letter writing are planned.
- **Entertainment:** The city lists all-ages performances, music and games.
- **Refreshments:** Hot chocolate and festive treats are part of the events.

Ladner's version is built around the village setting. Delta suggests pairing the lighting with food or treats from nearby local businesses.

## North Delta has skating and a $5 movie

North Delta has a few extra planning details. The city says visitors can use the public skate at North Delta Recreation Centre and buy dinner or snacks from the Jingle Bell Bites food area.

A holiday film at the North Delta Centre for the Arts is scheduled **after the event** and costs **$5**, with popcorn included. The tree lighting itself remains free, and the city says movie details are still to come.

## Before you go

- **Check the city page:** Programs and timing can change before November 26 and 28.
- **Dress for the weather:** Both events include outdoor activity.
- **Budget choice:** Entry is free; food, artisan purchases and North Delta's movie are optional costs.

The useful choice is location and day. Pick Thursday evening in Ladner for the village setting, or Saturday in North Delta if skating and the movie fit your plans.
