Surrey has a free community arts option on the calendar for Friday night. [The City of Surrey lists](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/clayton-coffee-house) the next Clayton Coffee House for **Friday, July 24, 2026**, from **6 to 7:30 p.m.**

The event is at the Performing Arts Studio inside Clayton Community Centre, at **7155 187A Street**. Surrey describes it as an all-ages, drop-in event where residents can watch local artists perform.

## What the event is

Clayton Coffee House is a monthly performance night for local poets, spoken-word artists, novelists, musicians, improvisers, comedians, and other performers.

Surrey says admission is free, but capacity is limited. The practical move is to arrive with enough time to find the studio and avoid treating the seat count as guaranteed.

## Who can perform

The city says performers are given **5 to 8 minutes**, with limited drop-in spots available on show day. Surrey's current page says applications for the September 11 event are due by September 6, so the July 24 event is mainly a go-watch plan unless a drop-in spot remains.

The broader City of Surrey community-art update also lists Clayton Coffee House as part of its summer arts lineup, alongside Sunday family art drop-ins through August 23 and fall-program registration opening July 26.

## What to check before going

Because this is a short evening event, check Surrey's event page before leaving for any schedule or capacity update. If you are planning around children, transit, or dinner nearby, the key details are simple: Friday night, Clayton Community Centre, free admission, and limited capacity.
