[The City of Surrey's event listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/thursday-artist-talk-panjabi-tour-keerat-kaur) says a free Thursday Artist Talk with Keerat Kaur is scheduled at Surrey Art Gallery on July 30.

The practical value is simple: residents who want a free evening arts event can confirm the language focus, time, address, and no-registration note before deciding whether to go.

## What is confirmed

The City lists the event details this way:

- **Date**: Thursday, July 30, 2026
- **Time**: 7 to 8:30 p.m.
- **Price**: Free
- **Location**: Surrey Art Gallery
- **Address**: 13750 88 Ave.

The listing describes the program as a guided exhibition tour in Panjabi. It also says no registration is required.

## What happens during the tour

The City says the Surrey Art Gallery Association is hosting the evening. The program begins with a live performance by Kaur, then moves into a tour of her exhibition, *If Gardens Could Dream*, in Panjabi.

That makes the listing most useful for gallery visitors who want both an artist-led program and a language-specific tour rather than a general exhibition visit.

The event page says Kaur will speak about:

- **Panjabi language and Gurmukhi script**: Why celebrating both matters, and why preserving the language is central to her work.
- **Artistic practice**: The source says Kaur's talk connects illustration, poetry, music, and design.
- **Exhibition context**: The tour focuses on Kaur's exhibition, *If Gardens Could Dream*.

The page also says Associate Curator Suvi Bains will lead portions of the tour in English, giving additional context on the exhibition.

## How to plan

The cleanest plan is to use the Surrey Art Gallery address, arrive before the **7 p.m.** start, and treat the event as a focused 90-minute gallery visit rather than a drop-in festival.

Before leaving, check the City page for any last-minute update. The current listing gives residents enough to decide: free admission, no registration, one evening only, and a clear language-specific tour format.
