[The City of Surrey lists](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/family-scavenger-hunt-darts-hill) a free **Family Scavenger Hunt at Darts Hill** for Sunday, **July 26, 2026**, giving South Surrey families a same-day outdoor plan inside one of the city's feature gardens.

The event runs from **10 am to 4 pm** at **Darts Hill Garden Park**, 1633 170 Street. Surrey says the activity sends families through garden paths and hidden corners while children learn about plants found in the park.

## What is confirmed

The city page gives enough planning detail for families deciding whether to go today.

- **Date**: Sunday, July 26, 2026
- **Time**: 10 am to 4 pm
- **Place**: Darts Hill Garden Park
- **Address**: 1633 170 Street, Surrey
- **Cost**: Free

Surrey describes the scavenger hunt as a family activity for all ages. The page says it is offered on the last Sunday of each month.

The event page places it under children, parks, and environment programming.

## Who it fits

This is a practical fit for families looking for an outdoor stop in South Surrey, especially if they want a park activity that gives children something specific to look for. The city's [Darts Hill Garden Park page](https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/darts-hill-garden-park) says the 7.5-acre feature garden includes plants from around the world and was gifted to Surrey residents in 1994.

## What to check before leaving

Surrey also lists **August 30** and **September 27** scavenger hunts, both from **10 am to 4 pm**, on the same event page.

Before heading out, check the city event page for any late update and plan for garden walking conditions. The park page points visitors to the garden page for operating hours and says charcoal briquette barbecues are not permitted in parks at this time.
