[The City of Surrey lists](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/family-scavenger-hunt-darts-hill) a **Family Scavenger Hunt at Darts Hill** for Sunday, **July 26, 2026**, giving South Surrey families a same-day outdoor plan inside one of the city's feature gardens.

The event runs from **10 am to 4 pm** at **Darts Hill Garden Park**, 1633 170 Street. The event listing says families follow garden paths and hidden corners while children learn about plants found in the park.

## Where and when to go

The city page gives enough planning detail for families deciding whether to go today.

- **Date**: Sunday, July 26, 2026
- **Time**: 10 am to 4 pm
- **Place**: Darts Hill Garden Park
- **Address**: 1633 170 Street, Surrey
- **Scavenger hunt**: Listed as free
- **Garden admission**: By donation, with a suggested $5 for general admission

The City and Darts Hill garden pages say admission is collected at the welcome table by cash or card. Children 12 and under can enter free with an adult, and Garden Members also enter free.

The event page places it under children, parks, and environment programming.

## Who it fits

This is a practical fit for families looking for an outdoor stop in South Surrey, especially if they want a park activity that gives children something specific to look for. The city's [Darts Hill Garden Park page](https://www.surrey.ca/parks-recreation/parks/park-features-amenities/display-feature-gardens/darts-hill-garden-park) says the garden includes plants from around the world.

Francisca and Edwin Darts donated the original 7.5-acre garden to Surrey residents in 1994. The City says the park now has 7.5 acres of historic gardens and facilities, with another 7 acres to explore.

## What to check before leaving

The July 26 event is the immediate planning window. Surrey also lists **August 30** and **September 27** scavenger hunts, both from **10 am to 4 pm**, on the same event page.

Before heading out, check the city event page for any late update and plan for garden walking conditions. The [Darts Hill visit page](https://dartshill.ca/visit-the-garden/) says there is no nearby ATM, so visitors should bring cash or card if they plan to make the suggested admission donation.
