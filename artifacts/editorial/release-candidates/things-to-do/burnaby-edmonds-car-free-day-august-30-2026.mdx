Burnaby is transforming Edmonds Street into a vibrant car-free festival zone on **Sunday, August 30, 2026**. The free event offers families and residents a full afternoon of live entertainment, food, activities and community connection without the need for a vehicle.

## Burnaby’s Car Free Day will close Edmonds Street and four nearby roads from 5 am to 10 pm on August 30 while hosting a free festival from noon to 5 pm that features multiple performance stages, local food vendors, family games and dance parties. Transit users should prepare for detours on the 106 route with additional service from Edmonds SkyTrain Station (per event page).

The City of Burnaby and presenting partner TransLink created the event to encourage sustainable transportation and neighbourhood connection. Detailed planning information comes directly from the official city news release and event page.

---

## Full Festival Lineup and Activities

The main stage and family stage will host a packed schedule of performers. Key highlights include:

- **Noon**: Event start with Stories on Wheels and Lili Flamenco dance (12:07 pm)
- **12:40 pm**: Las Divinas (Afro-Caribbean Folklore Dance)
- **12:55 pm**: Giordano dance performance
- **1:00 pm**: Official ceremony
- **1:28 pm**: Los Timberos
- **1:34 pm**: Nico Rana
- **1:41 pm**: Wailele Wai Wai
- **1:59 pm**: Appare Yosakoi
- **2:49 pm**: Anahaw Filipino
- **2:58 pm**: Pasión Venezuela
- **3:23 pm**: Jessica
- **4:02 pm**: African Masters

Family Stage includes 2:00 Ira Pettle DJ Dance Party. The Community Pop Up hosted by Yunik Dance includes a distinct 2-3 pm Random Play Dance (two distinct programmed items per event page). Additional family activities, games and community booths available throughout the afternoon.

---

## Road Closures, Parking Rules and Safety

Five streets will close to traffic from **5 am to 10 pm** on August 30:

- **Edmonds Street**: Kingsway to Mary Avenue
- **Humphries Avenue**: to Elwell Street (news release); to Vista Crescent (event page)
- **Linden Avenue**: 7216 Linden to Edmonds Street (no underground parking access)
- **Noble Lane**: Driveway at 7303 Noble Lane to Edmonds Street
- **Fulton Avenue**: Mid-block to Edmonds Street

Parking on the streets noted will be restricted to “No Parking” or “Special Event Parking” between 11:59 pm on August 29 to 12:01 pm on August 31. In addition, no parking on the north side of 19th Ave between Kingsway and Mary Ave from 6 am to 10 pm on August 30. Vehicles left will be towed. See official map.

Safety measures include portable accessible washrooms, hand sanitization stations, roving first aid staff, lost and found at the info tent, and bike valet on Noble Lane. The event is smoke-free and prohibits alcohol, weapons, animals (except service dogs), tents, frisbees, musical instruments and more. Edmonds Community Centre will be open for cooling if temperatures rise.

---

## Transit Detours and How to Get There

Bus routes **106 Edmonds** and **106 New West** will follow detours via Canada Way, 10th Avenue and Kingsway. Extra service will run from Edmonds SkyTrain Station directly to the event during festival hours.

Visitors should use public transit, biking, rideshare or off-site parking. The site is approximately a 10-minute bus ride or 25-minute walk from Edmonds SkyTrain. TransLink's trip planner is recommended for real-time updates.

---

## Practical Tips for Attendees

Key guidelines to make your visit smooth:

- **What to bring**: Reusable water bottle (refill stations available), weather-appropriate clothing, comfortable shoes. Prepare food in advance if bringing snacks (no knives allowed).
- **What not to bring**: Alcohol, outside drinks with alcohol, weapons, tents, tarps, umbrellas, balls, frisbees, animals, lasers, fireworks or musical instruments.
- **Accessibility**: Multiple accessible washrooms, drop-off area at Edmonds Community Centre surface lot for SPARC users, cooling centre available.
- **Bike valet**: Bike valet on Noble Lane just east of Kingsway (per event page).
- **Rain or shine**: The event runs regardless of weather. Check for extreme heat notifications.

The event promotes Burnaby's neighbourhood identity and sustainable living. Full details and the latest updates are on the official Burnaby Car Free Day page.
