[The City of Burnaby says](https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-08-14/burnaby-launches-car-free-day-festivities-edmonds) it is partnering with TransLink on **Car Free Day**, a free street festival that encourages residents to leave their cars at home and explore other ways of getting around.

The festival runs from **noon to 5 pm** on **Sunday, August 30, 2026**, on Edmonds Street between Kingsway and Mary Avenue.

## Event details

The city's release gives these core planning details:

- **Date**: Sunday, August 30, 2026
- **Time**: Noon to 5 pm
- **Where**: Edmonds Street, from Kingsway to Mary Avenue
- **Admission**: Free
- **On site**: Live entertainment including a family stage, family activities and games, local food and drinks, and community booths

## Road closures and parking

The city says traffic will not be permitted on these streets from **5 am to 10 pm** on August 30:

- **Edmonds Street**: from Kingsway to Mary Avenue
- **Humphries Avenue**: from 19th Avenue to Elwell Street
- **Linden Avenue**: from 7216 Linden to Edmonds Street, including no access at Edmonds Street and Linden to underground parking for nearby commercial or residential buildings
- **Noble Lane**: from the driveway at 7303 Noble Lane to Edmonds Street
- **Fulton Avenue**: from mid-block of Fulton Avenue to Edmonds Street

Parking on those streets is restricted to "No Parking" from **11:59 pm on August 29** to **12:01 pm on August 31**, according to the city. Burnaby has published a [site map](https://burnaby.widen.net/s/cbfxzjnkrd/car-free-day-site-map-horizontal) showing the closures.

## Bus detours

Transit riders should expect these changes on August 30, per the city:

- **106 Edmonds**: regular route to Edmonds and Canada Way, then via Canada Way, onto 10th Avenue, Kingsway, and back onto Edmonds for the regular route
- **106 New West**: regular route to Edmonds and Kingsway, then via Kingsway, onto 10th Avenue, Canada Way, and back onto Edmonds for the regular route
- Additional bus service is planned from Edmonds SkyTrain Station to the event during event hours
