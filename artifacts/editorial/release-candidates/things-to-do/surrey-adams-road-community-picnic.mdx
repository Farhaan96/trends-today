[The City of Surrey's event listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/community-picnic-adams-road-park) says a free Community Picnic at Adams Road Park is scheduled for Thursday, July 30.

The practical decision is whether to plan a casual early-evening park visit around picnic time, activities, games, and City partners on site. The official page says the first **200** people at each picnic can receive a free food item.

## What is confirmed

The City lists the core event details this way:

- **Date**: Thursday, July 30, 2026
- **Time**: 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.
- **Price**: Free
- **Location**: Adams Road Park
- **Address**: 18160 68 Ave.

The listing describes the event as a chance to get outdoors with friends and family, bring a picnic meal, learn about City services, and celebrate summer.

## What families can expect

The City says visitors can pack a picnic and join community networking, activities, and games. It also points attendees to Park Play, which offers self-directed park activities for families.

Planning notes from the official page:

- **Bring your own picnic** if you want more than the limited food-item offer.
- **Arrive early** if the free food-item note matters to you, because it applies to the first 200 people.
- **Expect outdoor games** and borrowed sports equipment through Park Play.
- **Use the official listing** for any same-day schedule change before leaving.

## Who is listed on site

The City names several City and community partners on the event page, including the Museum of Surrey, Surrey Animal Resource Centre, Surrey Bylaws, Surrey Mobile Library, Surrey Police Service, Bells & Whistles Mini-golf, Surrey Central Lions Club, the Salmon Habitat Restoration Program, and The Tennis Centre.

Before heading out, use the City listing to confirm the date, time, address, and free-price note still match your plan.
