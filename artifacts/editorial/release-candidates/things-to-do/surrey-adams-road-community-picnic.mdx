[The City of Surrey's event listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/community-picnic-adams-road-park) says a free Community Picnic at Adams Road Park is scheduled this week.

For Surrey families, the useful value is a low-commitment park evening: bring food, let children try self-directed games through Park Play, and arrive early if the limited free food item matters to your group.

## What is confirmed

The City lists the core event details this way:

- **Date**: Thursday, July 30, 2026
- **Time**: 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.
- **Price**: Free
- **Location**: Adams Road Park
- **Address**: 18160 68 Ave.

The listing describes the event as a chance to get outdoors with friends and family, bring a picnic meal, learn about City services, and celebrate summer.

## What families can expect

The City says visitors can pack a picnic and join community networking, activities, and games. It also points attendees to Park Play, which offers self-directed park activities for families.

Planning notes from the official page:

- **Bring your own picnic** if you want more than the limited food-item offer.
- **Arrive near the start** if the free food-item note matters to you.
- **Expect outdoor games** and borrowed sports equipment through Park Play.
- **Look for City and community partners** if you want to connect with local services while there.

## Who is listed on site

The City names several City and community partners on the event page. The list includes the Museum of Surrey, Surrey Animal Resource Centre, Surrey Bylaws, Surrey Mobile Library, Surrey Police Service, Bells & Whistles Mini-golf, Surrey Central Lions Club, the Salmon Habitat Restoration Program, and The Tennis Centre.

The cleanest plan is to pack a simple picnic, aim for the opening window if the limited food item matters, and use the Adams Road Park address before leaving.
