[The City of Burnaby says](https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/events/burnaby-blues-roots-festival) **Burnaby Blues + Roots Festival** is free on Saturday, **Aug. 8, 2026**, with music running from **3:30 to 10 pm** at the Deer Lake Park Festival Lawn.

The city lists free admission, food trucks, a beer garden, a community stage, satellite stages at Burnaby Village Museum, and a main-stage lineup led by TALK, Jully Black, Galactic featuring Jelly Joseph, Wyatt C. Louis, and TYA.

## What is confirmed

The core planning details are fixed enough to decide whether to go.

- **Date**: Saturday, Aug. 8, 2026
- **Main site**: Deer Lake Park Festival Lawn, 6450 Deer Lake Avenue
- **Festival time**: 3:30 to 10 pm
- **Admission**: Free
- **Nearby daytime music**: Burnaby Village Museum satellite stages run from 11:30 am to 3:15 pm

The main stage starts with TYA at **4 pm**, then Wyatt C. Louis at **4:45 pm**, Galactic featuring Jelly Joseph at **5:40 pm**, Jully Black at **7:15 pm**, and TALK from **8:30 to 10 pm**.

## How to get there

The biggest practical note is parking. The city says there is no public on-site parking except accessible parking for guests with SPARC passes, space allowing, in the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts surface lot.

Burnaby points visitors toward transit, cycling, rideshare, taxis, carpooling, or off-site parking at Burnaby Central Secondary, Burnaby City Hall, and Deer Lake Centre lots. The city lists the **144, 110, 123, and 133** buses as routes that stop outside the site, but says transit vehicles will be redirected to Canada Way and the City Hall bus loop after **9 pm**.

Anyone taking transit should check their route before leaving, especially if they plan to leave after the 9 pm redirect.

## Food, access, and site rules

The city says portable washrooms, including accessible washrooms, will be available at the festival. A ramped accessible seating platform is planned, and the free Bettear app is recommended for personalized audio accessibility at the main stage.

The city says food trucks and a beer garden will be on site. Visitors can also bring their own food and non-alcoholic drinks.

Key rules to plan around:

- **Allowed**: Lawn chairs and outside food or non-alcoholic drinks
- **Not allowed**: Outside alcohol, knives, tents, tarps, umbrellas, pets, drones, fireworks, smoking, vaping, and professional recording equipment
- **Accessibility**: Accessible parking is limited to SPARC-pass guests, space allowing
- **Cycling**: A free bike valet is planned on site

## Why it matters for August plans

The tradeoff is simple: the event is free, but arrival planning matters because regular public parking is off site.

The useful move is to pick one anchor set, then choose an arrival path. Come before 3:15 pm if the Burnaby Village Museum stages are part of the plan, arrive before the 5:40 pm Galactic set for the main evening run, or treat the 8:30 pm TALK set as the late anchor. For another free city-run option before August, Trends Today's [Burnaby Art in the Park dates](/things-to-do/burnaby-art-in-park-making-together) bulletin lists drop-in park sessions starting July 27.
