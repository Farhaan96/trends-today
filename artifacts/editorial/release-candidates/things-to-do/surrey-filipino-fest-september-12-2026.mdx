Surrey’s **Filipino Fest** returns to Museum of Surrey on Saturday, **Sept. 12**, with a free program of performances, food and activities for visitors of all ages.

The [City of Surrey notice](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/filipino-fest-returns-museum-of-surrey) says the family-friendly festival runs from **11 a.m. to 4 p.m.** at the museum.

## Date, time and location

The essential planning details are:

- **Date**: Saturday, Sept. 12
- **Time**: 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.
- **Place**: Museum of Surrey, 17710 56A Ave.
- **Admission**: Free

The City describes Filipino Fest as a chance to celebrate, discover and learn about Filipino culture through engagement and interaction. It is open to people reconnecting with their heritage and those experiencing Filipino culture for the first time. The program brings food, music, performances, activities and vendors together at the museum.

## Performances and activities

The entertainment lineup named by the City includes Glisha, The Kuyas, Raffy Swap One, Red Filos, Mabuhay Arnis and Grupo Ilocano Association of BC.

Visitors can also expect several ways to take part throughout the day:

- Cultural performances, traditional music and dance
- Cultural displays, photo opportunities and textile exhibits
- Children’s activities and artisan vendors

Museum of Surrey Manager Lynn Saffery said the museum is working with the Filipino community as participants share traditions through performance, food, activities, vendors and music.

## Food at the festival

The City says Filipino food vendors and food trucks will offer flavours from across the Philippines. Its notice names these vendors:

- Cake It Easy
- Nanay Kitchen
- Dale’s Food Products
- Kasalo
- Timplado Filipino Street Food Truck

The practical next step is to note the museum address and arrive between 11 a.m. and 4 p.m. The City directs people seeking more information to its Museum of Surrey website.
