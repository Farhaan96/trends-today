Burnaby's next Walk and Chat with the Mayor is scheduled for **Sunday, July 26**, giving residents one more community-walk date before August.

The City of Burnaby lists the walk from **9 to 10 a.m.** at Christine Sinclair Community Centre, at **3713 Kensington Avenue**. The city says the walks run rain or shine.

## What happens on Sunday

The city describes the event as a healthy walk and chat with Mayor Hurley. Participants meet at Christine Sinclair Community Centre before the walk begins.

Burnaby says a **$2 pancake breakfast** is served at **10 a.m.** by the Burnaby Firefighters Charitable Society, after the walking portion.

## The remaining dates

Burnaby lists the walks for the final Sunday of each month from March through August. The remaining dates on the event page are:

- **Sunday, July 26**: 9 to 10 a.m.
- **Sunday, August 30**: 9 to 10 a.m.

That makes July 26 the next listed walk, with August 30 as the remaining summer date on the city page.

## What to check before going

The main decision is whether the timing fits a Sunday morning. The city lists the location phone number as **604-320-2222** for Christine Sinclair Community Centre.

Because the city says the walks happen rain or shine, check the weather and dress for a comfortable hour outside. If you are planning around breakfast, note that the city lists it at 10 a.m., after the 9 to 10 a.m. walk.
