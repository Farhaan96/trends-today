[The City of Surrey's event listing](https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/events/sounds-of-summer-boot-scooters-line-dancing) says **Sounds of Summer: The Boot Scooters & Line Dancing** is a free outdoor concert at Surrey Civic Plaza on Wednesday, July 29.

The event is part of Surrey's Sounds of Summer Concert Series. The City describes it as an interactive country concert with line-dancing instruction, so the practical decision is simple: go if you want a free evening event where beginners can take part instead of only watching.

## What is confirmed for July 29

The official listing gives these core details:

- **Date**: Wednesday, July 29, 2026
- **Time**: 6:30 to 8 p.m.
- **Price**: Free, with no ticket required
- **Venue**: Surrey Civic Plaza
- **Address**: 13450 104 Avenue

The City names The Boot Scooters as the featured act. It says the group includes touring session musicians and professional dancers, with line-dancing and two-stepping instruction built into the show.

## What to bring and expect

The City says seating is limited and tells visitors to bring their own chair. It also says washrooms are available at Surrey City Hall.

Planning notes from the official page:

- **Parking**: Paid parking is available in the Surrey City Hall parkade.
- **Pets**: The City recommends leaving pets at home because concert sites can be crowded.
- **Accessibility**: Service animals are permitted, with accessible parking and accessible washrooms at Surrey City Hall.
- **Food**: Visitors can bring a picnic but should pack out waste and sort it at home.
- **Alcohol**: Alcohol is not permitted in unlicensed public spaces.

## Last-minute checklist

Before leaving, the key checks are practical:

- **Bring a chair** because the City says seating is limited.
- **Use the City Hall parkade** if you plan to drive and are comfortable with paid parking.
- **Leave pets at home** unless you are bringing a service animal.
- **Recheck the City page** for same-day changes because this is an outdoor event.
