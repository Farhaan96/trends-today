Coquitlam drivers should expect daytime delays on **Como Lake Avenue** this week. The city says **BC Hydro utility work** starts Wednesday, July 29, between **Clarke Road and North Road**, with traffic narrowed during active construction.

The notice does not list a full closure. It does say the route will be reduced to **one lane in each direction** when crews are working.

## What changes on Como Lake Avenue

During active construction, traffic will keep moving in both directions, according to the City of Coquitlam notice.

The trade-off is space. The city says the work zone will operate with one lane each way, and drivers should expect traffic personnel, posted signs, and construction speed limits.

## Dates and route to watch

The notice gives a specific daytime work window:

- **When**: Wednesday, July 29, to Friday, Aug. 7.
- **Hours**: 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. during active work.
- **Excluded dates**: Sunday, Aug. 2, and Monday, Aug. 3.

Those hours matter because the work is listed for the middle of the day. A morning or evening trip outside the active window may avoid the lane squeeze, based on the schedule in the city notice.

## What drivers should do

Coquitlam is asking drivers to plan an alternate route to avoid delays or to add extra time when using this part of Como Lake Avenue.

The city says drivers should travel with caution through the construction zone.

The practical move is simple:

- Use another route if the trip is time-sensitive.
- Leave extra time if you must travel during the **9 a.m. to 3 p.m.** work window.
- Watch for crews, equipment, signs, and traffic personnel.

For project questions during the work window, Coquitlam lists Engineering and Public Works customer service at **604-927-3500** and **epw@coquitlam.ca**.
