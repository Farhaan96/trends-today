Drivers using **Guildford Way** at Falcon Drive or Lansdowne Drive should plan for delays while intersection safety improvements are underway.

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2024) says its contractor began work August 10. The project is expected to take about one month to complete, weather dependent.

## Where the work is happening

The project covers two intersections on Guildford Way:

- **Guildford Way at Falcon Drive**
- **Guildford Way at Lansdowne Drive**

Coquitlam describes the project as intersection safety improvements. The notice does not list specific design changes.

## When crews may be working

The City lists these construction hours:

- **Monday to Friday**: 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.
- **Possible Saturday work**: 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.

The stated duration is approximate and depends on weather.

## What drivers should expect

Coquitlam says traffic flow will be affected during construction and some delays are expected. The City advises drivers to use an alternate route or allow extra time.

That advice applies during the listed construction hours at both intersections. The notice does not announce a full road closure or give a day-by-day traffic plan, so drivers should rely on construction signs and traffic personnel when they reach the work zone.

For people travelling through the construction zone, the notice lists three safety reminders:

- Drive with extreme caution and follow construction speed limits.
- Watch for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Obey construction signs and instructions from traffic personnel.

## How to contact the City

Questions can be directed to Coquitlam's Public Works Liaison at **604-927-3550** or **pwl@coquitlam.ca**.

The notice also directs residents to the City's Facebook and Instagram accounts for traffic-hotspot updates. Anyone planning a trip through the area can check those official channels, allow extra time and then follow the instructions posted at the construction zone.
