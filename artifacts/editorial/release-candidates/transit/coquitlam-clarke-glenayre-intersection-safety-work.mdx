Drivers using **Clarke Road at Glenayre Drive** should plan for delays while intersection safety improvements are underway.

The [City of Coquitlam notice](https://www.coquitlam.ca/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=2020) says the contractor's work was scheduled to begin during the week of August 10. The project was scheduled to continue for approximately one month.

## Where the work is happening

The project is at the intersection of **Clarke Road and Glenayre Drive** in Coquitlam.

Coquitlam describes the project as intersection safety improvements. The notice does not identify specific design changes, so the scope should not be described more narrowly than the City's wording.

## When crews may be working

The City lists one construction schedule:

- **Monday to Friday**: 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

The notice says work was scheduled to begin during the week of August 10 and continue for approximately one month. It does not list Saturday hours or describe the schedule as weather dependent.

## What drivers should expect

Coquitlam says traffic flow will be affected and delays will occur during active construction. The City asks drivers to plan an alternate route to avoid delays or allow extra time when using the route.

The notice also gives three reminders for travelling through the construction zone:

- Drive with extreme caution and follow construction speed limits.
- Watch closely for work crews and heavy machinery.
- Obey construction signs and instructions from traffic personnel.

Drivers can check the official notice before travelling, allow extra time and follow directions posted at the work zone.

## How to contact the City

Questions can be directed to Coquitlam's Public Works Liaison at **604-927-3550** or **pwl@coquitlam.ca**.

The notice also points residents to the City's Facebook and Instagram accounts for traffic-hotspot updates. Those official channels may provide updates while the project is active.
