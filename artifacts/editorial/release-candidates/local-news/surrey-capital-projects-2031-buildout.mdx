Surrey says Newton Community Centre site preparation is expected to be complete this summer, a major 72 Avenue extension runs toward **2031**, and two downtown City Centre projects are moving through late-decade timelines.

The city highlighted the four projects on **July 24, 2026**, saying they are part of more than **$1.6 billion** in major capital projects. The update points residents to Newton Community Centre, the 72 Avenue corridor, Centre Block, and City Centre Arena.

For anyone tracking construction, growth, or neighbourhood access, the useful detail is that these are staged projects. Some work is already underway, while other pieces are still moving through design, permitting, or agreement stages.

## Where Surrey says work is moving

Surrey's July 24 update frames the four projects as major investments in recreation, transportation, and city-building. The city says the work is intended to support a growing population and create more places to live, work, gather, and travel.

The four projects are:

- **Newton Community Centre**: a new recreation, culture, childcare, and library facility near Newton Arena.
- **72 Avenue improvements and extension**: road upgrades and a planned extension from 152 Street to Highway 15.
- **Centre Block**: a two-phase downtown office, retail, and academic development beside Surrey Central Station.
- **City Centre Arena**: a planned 10,000-seat arena and public plaza near King George Boulevard and 104 Avenue.

For related neighbourhood recreation work already covered by Trends Today, see [Surrey's Newton park upgrades](/local-news/surrey-newton-park-upgrades).

## Newton Community Centre has a 2030 target

The city says the new Newton Community Centre is budgeted at **$310.6 million** and planned as a **175,000-square-foot** facility. It is slated for the east side of King George Boulevard, south of the existing Newton Arena and near Newton Seniors Centre.

Surrey says the centre will include a 50-metre pool, leisure pool, hot tub, cold pool, two full-sized gymnasiums, fitness amenities, arts and childcare spaces, multi-purpose rooms, and an expanded library.

The project timeline lists site preparation complete and early works commencing in **summer 2026**, with construction completion expected in **spring 2030**.

## 72 Avenue work reaches into 2031

The 72 Avenue project is the road piece of Surrey's July 24 update. The city says the corridor is intended to improve east-west travel and link Newton, Fleetwood, Clayton, and Cloverdale.

Current project updates split the work into several segments:

- **144 Street to 152 Street**: construction is underway, with completion anticipated in 2027.
- **152 Street to Highway 15**: the first phase of preloading from 152 Street to Bear Creek is underway, with the new road anticipated to be complete in 2031.
- **188 Street to 196 Street**: parking work has been completed on several side streets, with construction moving eastward from 188 Street and completion anticipated in fall 2026.

That means the corridor is not one single short-term closure or opening. Residents should expect a multi-year buildout with different timelines by segment.

## Downtown projects are still taking shape

Centre Block and City Centre Arena are the two downtown Surrey projects in the update.

For Centre Block, Surrey says phase one will include a **12-storey** building with ground-floor shops, office space, a shared lobby, and the future SFU School of Medicine. The city says phase one construction is expected to begin in **late 2026**, with completion expected in **2030**.

City Centre Arena is earlier in the process. Surrey says definitive agreements and design and permitting are expected in **2026/2027**, construction is expected to begin in **2027**, and arena construction is expected to complete in **2029/2030**.

The city says the arena plan starts with a **10,000-seat** arena and public plaza. Future phases led by private-sector partners are expected to include a hotel, restaurants, a coffee shop, spa, banquet space, residential towers, ground-floor retail, potential convention space, and additional entertainment amenities.
