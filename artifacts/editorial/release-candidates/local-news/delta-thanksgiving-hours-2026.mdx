**Thanksgiving Monday falls on October 12, 2026**, and Delta residents have three separate schedules to track. Civic buildings do not all follow the same hours, curbside collection moves one day later for the rest of the week, and regional transit switches to holiday service.

The practical rule is simple: check the facility before leaving home, shift your curbside day by one day, and use TransLink’s holiday schedule for Monday.

---

## Which Delta facilities are open on Thanksgiving?

Delta City Hall and the Delta Community Animal Shelter are closed on Monday. The North Delta Recreation Centre remains open for its regular hours, but the Municipal Services Office inside it is closed.

That distinction matters if your trip combines recreation with City business. An open building does not mean the Municipal Services Office inside it is available. Plan recreation from the facility hours, and leave City Hall business for a regular business day.

Delta says its Parks, Recreation and Culture facilities are open for regular hours. The Douglas J. Husband Discovery Centre has a specific holiday schedule and is open from **noon to 5 p.m.**

Several community facilities are closed for the holiday:

- **Tsawwassen Arts Centre**: Closed Monday
- **North Delta Centre for the Arts**: Closed Monday
- **McKee Seniors Recreation Centre**: Closed Monday
- **Kennedy Seniors Recreation Centre**: Closed Monday

---

## When is garbage and recycling collected?

Delta’s statutory-holiday rule delays curbside collection by one day for the rest of Thanksgiving week. Materials still need to be at the curb by **7 a.m.** on the revised day.

The City lists these changes when Monday is a statutory holiday:

- **Monday collection**: Tuesday, October 13
- **Wednesday collection**: Thursday, October 15
- **Thursday collection**: Friday, October 16

Regular collection days resume the following week. This shift applies to garbage, recycling and Green Can materials on the City’s collection schedule.

---

## How does TransLink run on October 12?

Bus, SkyTrain and SeaBus operate on a **Sunday or holiday schedule** on Thanksgiving Monday. West Coast Express does not operate.

Holiday fares are also in effect. TransLink says a **one-zone fare** is enough for travel across all zones that day. Regular weekday service and fares return Tuesday, October 13.

TransLink’s Customer Service Centre, Access Transit Customer Care Office and Lost Property Office are closed Monday. Riders should check the Trip Planner or TransLink’s active service notices before leaving.

---

## A quick plan for Monday

Confirm a facility’s hours before making the trip. If Monday is your normal curbside day, put materials out for Tuesday by 7 a.m. Transit riders should plan around Sunday-level frequency and the absence of West Coast Express.

The schedules come from three official pages and were current when checked on October 10. Use the linked agency pages to confirm the latest notice before leaving.
