Richmond residents now have the main voting dates for the city's **2026 General Local and School Election**. General Voting Day is **Saturday, October 17**, and advance voting is scheduled across five dates in early October.

The election will choose **one mayor**, **eight councillors** and **seven school board trustees**. The practical step for voters is to check registration, voting-place and ballot-access options before the campaign gets noisy.

## Key voting dates Richmond lists

The city says registering in advance helps make voting quicker and easier because registered voters will receive a voter card in the mail with their voting place.

Important dates from Richmond's notice:

- **August 18**: Resident-elector advance registration through Elections BC.
- **September 15**: Mail-ballot online applications open, according to Richmond's elections page.
- **October 3, 7, 8, 9 and 10**: Advance-voting dates.
- **October 17**: General Voting Day for the local and school election.

## What residents can check now

Richmond says eligible residents can use the city's "Am I on the Voters List?" tool to confirm whether they are already registered. The city also lists advance voting places and a "Where Do I Vote?" tool for General Voting Day.

Voters may also register when voting by bringing two separate pieces of ID that together include their name and residential address. One piece must include a signature. Non-resident property electors are directed to contact the Richmond Elections Office before voting.

## Accessible voting options are included

The city says several accessible voting options will be available for this election. The listed options include:

- **Curb-side voting** for voters who need that accommodation.
- **Mail ballots** for voters who cannot or prefer not to vote in person.
- **An accessible ballot-marking device** at City Hall during advance voting.

Voters may cast only **one ballot**, according to the city notice.

## What to do next

Use Richmond's elections page now to check the voters list, find advance voting places or look up the designated General Voting Day location. If you plan to register at the voting place, bring the required ID.

Voters who need help at the voting place can call the Elections Office when they arrive to request curb-side voting. Mail-ballot applications open online on September 15, according to the city page.
