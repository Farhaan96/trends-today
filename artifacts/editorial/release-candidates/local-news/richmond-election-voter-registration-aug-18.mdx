Richmond residents now have the main voting dates for the city's **2026 General Local and School Election**. General Voting Day is **Saturday, October 17**, and resident electors can register in advance through Elections BC until **Tuesday, August 18**.

That August date is for early registration, not the last possible way to register. Richmond's elections page says voters can also register when they vote if they bring the required identification.

The election will choose **one mayor**, **eight councillors** and **seven school board trustees**. The immediate step for voters is to check whether they are on the voters list and decide whether early registration will make voting faster.

## Key dates Richmond voters should know

The city says registering in advance helps make voting quicker and easier because registered voters will receive a voter card in the mail with their voting place.

Important dates from Richmond's notice:

- **Aug. 18**: Early registration date Richmond is pointing resident electors to through Elections BC.
- **October 3**: First listed advance-voting day.
- **October 7, 8, 9 and 10**: Additional advance-voting days.
- **Oct. 17**: General Voting Day for the local and school election.

## What residents can check now

Richmond says eligible residents can use the city's "Am I on the Voters List?" tool to confirm whether they are already registered. Resident electors can register in advance through Elections BC until August 18.

The city's election page says voters may also register when voting by bringing two separate pieces of ID that together include their name and residential address. One piece must include a signature. Non-resident property electors are directed to contact the Richmond Elections Office before voting.

## Accessible voting options are included

The city says several accessible voting options will be available for this election. The listed options include:

- **Curb-side voting** for voters who need that accommodation.
- **Mail ballots** for voters who cannot or prefer not to vote in person.
- **An accessible ballot-marking device** at City Hall during advance voting.

Voters may cast only **one ballot**, according to the city notice.

## What to do next

Resident electors who want the faster voter-card path should register early by August 18. Voters planning to register at the voting place should bring the required ID, and non-resident property electors should contact the Richmond Elections Office before voting.

The next thing to watch is Richmond's elections page for assigned voting places, advance-voting locations and mail-ballot instructions.
