Richmond residents have a new election date to put on the calendar. The city says its **2026 General Local and School Election** will be held on **Saturday, October 17**, with voter registration encouraged before **Tuesday, August 18**.

The election will choose **one mayor**, **eight councillors** and **seven school board trustees**. The immediate step for voters is simple: check whether you are on the voters list and register early if you are not.

## Key dates Richmond voters should know

The city says registering in advance helps make voting quicker and easier because registered voters will receive a voter card in the mail with their voting place.

Important dates from Richmond's notice:

- **Aug. 18**: Deadline Richmond is pointing residents to for advance registration through Elections BC.
- **October 3**: First listed advance-voting day.
- **October 7, 8, 9 and 10**: Additional advance-voting days.
- **Oct. 17**: General Voting Day for the local and school election.

## What residents can check now

Richmond says eligible residents can use the city's "Am I on the Voters List?" tool to confirm whether they are already registered.

The city's election page is also the place to watch for voting-place details as the election gets closer. The July 28 notice says eligible voters can vote at their designated voting place on General Voting Day or at any advance-voting location during the five advance dates.

## Accessible voting options are included

The city says several accessible voting options will be available for this election. The listed options include:

- **Curb-side voting** for voters who need that accommodation.
- **Mail ballots** for voters who cannot or prefer not to vote in person.
- **An accessible ballot-marking device** at City Hall during advance voting.

Voters may cast only **one ballot**, according to the city notice.

## What to do next

Richmond voters who are unsure about registration should check the voters-list tool before August 18. After that, the practical thing to watch is the city's elections page for assigned voting places, advance-voting locations and any mail-ballot instructions.
