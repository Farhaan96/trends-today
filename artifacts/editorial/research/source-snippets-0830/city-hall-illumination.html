July 6, 2026
July 7, 2026
5:00 pm
5:00 am
City Hall, 511 Royal Avenue, New Westminster
This year, Monday July 6th, 2026, marks the 10th annual Canadian Injury Prevention Day; a day to raise awareness about preventable injuries and aid Canadians to live long lives to the fullest through education and advocacy.
